A shared world

What changes in multiplayer?

Your character. Your save. One shared adventure.

Save your character

Everyone keeps their own save. The server holds no replacement. Save often.

Keep one campaign leader

The same leader handles quest dialogue, scenes and items, and enters first. Everyone can fight and solve puzzles.

Menus stay live

Our setup does not pause. Get out of danger before opening menus.

Death means respawning

Normally respawn nearby outdoors or at the indoor entrance. Extra gold-loss penalty: zero. Crime bounties still apply. Exact party setup not tested yet.

Master for everyone

Master difficulty is enforced. Enemies do not scale with party size. Combat experience sharing excludes crafting.

Watch friendly NPCs

Player damage to players and killcams are off. Lock-on targets hostiles only. Followers and townsfolk can still be hurt.

Keep this campaign together

Keep characters together in progress. Use separate characters for other campaigns, including a smaller-party night. Finish Helgen offline before connecting.

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

Unsaved progress may be lost. Tell the group later; no recap required.

F2 means F two. This party's exit rehearsal is not tested yet.
