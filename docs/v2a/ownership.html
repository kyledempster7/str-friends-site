What belongs to you

Gear, gold and homes

Your save, pack and gold are personal.

Gear and loot

Offer upgrades before selling. Leader takes quest items. Stop if anything vanishes or duplicates.

Gold

No automatic party purse. Zero percentage death penalty still leaves crime fines.

Check home storage

Recognized home containers are intended to stay local. Test the exact chest with a cheap item, save and rejoin. No blanket privacy or permanence guarantee; your friend's chest is not your delivery box.

Meeting place, not bank

Shared containers are not permanent banks. Guild, outdoor and modded storage needs checks. Empty rooms before renovations remove containers.

Hand-off: not tested yet

Try one cheap item in an unowned container outside a home. Full menu: donor deposits and closes; recipient takes. Both check packs, save and rejoin. Never trade floor drops.

One person at a time. Stop on disagreement. Gold, crafted items and QuickLoot transfers remain not tested yet.

Buy a city home

Fixed leader completes prerequisites; buy from the steward. Budget separately for furnishings. Do not repeat quests alone.

Build with Hearthfire

Plots cost 5,000 gold before construction. Use the drafting table and carpenter's bench. Shared building is not tested yet.

Check each character

Nonleader payments, keys, access and furnishings are not tested yet. Promise neither one shared purchase nor four required payments.

Proposed ownership compact

Proposed etiquette, not accepted. If adopted: our agreement, not the game's rules.

  • Your gear and gold; no spawning, duplication or reload-farming.
  • Offer upgrades; alternate ties. No leader bonus.
  • Leader handles quest NPCs and items.
  • Shared costs voluntary; pledges aren't payments.
  • Name supply holders; test personal storage.
  • Earn homes; agree buyer and guest use.
  • Protect absent players' belongings and story.

Optional: name the company, share crafting interests, choose a home goal. Record supplies as pledged, held or spent.

Family systems, horse ownership after reconnecting and custom-item supply chains are not tested yet.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

Unsaved progress may be lost. Tell the group later; no recap required.

F2 means F two. This party's exit rehearsal is not tested yet.
