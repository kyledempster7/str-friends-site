Spells and magic

Our spell mod is Mysticism

Heard Apocalypse mentioned earlier? Our selected mod is Mysticism: familiar spells rebalanced, new choices, the same five schools.

Alteration

Vanilla: protection and utility. Mysticism adds enemy debuffs. Mark and Recall travel are held.

Conjuration

Vanilla: bound weapons, summons, raised corpses. Mysticism adds summons, including skeletons. One active summon per player; corpse raising held.

Destruction

Vanilla: elemental attacks. Mysticism expands progression and drain spells, including Vampiric Bolt and Blood Lance.

Illusion

Vanilla: stealth, morale, mind control. Mysticism expands choices. Enemy-control spells cannot control human players.

Restoration

Vanilla: healing, wards, undead defense. Mysticism expands support. Start with Healing Hands, Heal Other and Grand Healing.

Buy spell tomes from usual vendors. Staff builds are welcome; extra staff skill experience is not included.

Support isn't a completed test

Ordinary healing, Courage, Rally and Call to Arms have player-target support. Exact targeting, experience and perk scaling are not tested yet. Custom auras and undead healing are not automatically player heals.

Main Mysticism only

Hold Mark, Recall and corpse reanimation. Exclude NPC spell distribution and optional Jump, Survival, Ordinator and Slower Unlocks packages.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

Unsaved progress may be lost. Tell the group later; no recap required.

F2 means F two. This party's exit rehearsal is not tested yet.
