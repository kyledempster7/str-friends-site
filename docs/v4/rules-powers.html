What we've checked

NameTypeStatusWhat to know
Beast Tongue — Imperiousrace powerQuarantined — don't use yetThe Wood Elf power recruits an animal companion. It befriends the nearest carnivorous animal and allows one animal companion. Do not use this power in our campaign. Source
Contingency — Imperiousrace powerQuarantined — don't use yetThe High Elf power automatically casts stored spells. You choose a trigger and store equipped spells for free automatic casting. Do not use this power in our campaign. Source
Red Sand Dance — Imperiousrace powerQuarantined — don't use yetThe Redguard power slows time while sprinting. Its 45-second trance slows time by 90% during sprints. Do not activate this power in our campaign. Source
Spirit Walk — Imperiousrace powerQuarantined — don't use yetThe Dark Elf power summons two invulnerable ancestors. It spends two Spirit Favor for 45 seconds of ancestral help. Our ordinary summon allowance does not clear this power. Source
Discipline — Imperiousrace powerConditionalBoosts nearby allies' armor and attacks; do not rely on it protecting players. During armed combat, nearby allies gain armor and attack damage. Its effect on human players is not verified; it does not heal. Source
Harrier — Imperiousrace powerConditionalMarks prey for loot and weakens foes; do not rely on a party-wide bonus. The bird points out animals or enemies, reducing enemy armor and magic resistance. Benefits for friends and quest progress are not verified. Source
Prowl — Imperiousrace powerConditionalQuieter sneaking and a powerful leap. Leap while sneaking with weapons sheathed, spending Stamina. It is movement, not teleportation. Not tested in co-op yet; stay with the party. Source
Shared Ancestry — Imperiousrace powerConditionalBorrow a human or elf's racial power; all existing quarantines still apply. The Breton power borrows a living target's racial power for five minutes. Never use it to bypass quarantined powers. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Whispers — Imperiousrace powerConditionalAncestors mark enemies for gold and Spirit Favor. Slay marked humanoids to earn the reward. Personal progress is not tested in co-op yet; Spirit Walk remains quarantined (don't use yet). Source
