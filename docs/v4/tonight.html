04 · A night of play

A night of play, before and after

Joining

StepDo this
Before your first joinMatch the group's collection. Finish Helgen or Helgen Express offline and save outside.
1Launch Skyrim Together and load your latest campaign save.
2Press F2 or Right Ctrl. Choose Connect and use the host's private details.
3In Player Manager, join the party with the usual leader.
4No party yet? The leader creates it. Join before doing any quests.
5Meet up and say or type READY.
