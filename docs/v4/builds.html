Quick answer

QuestionShort answer
What should I play?Anything you enjoy. For ideas, see Build sheets and the Roster.
Suggestions using our installed mods. Co-op effects remain untested.
BuildSingle playerCo-op
Two-handed guardianProtect yourself and your followerTwo-handed, Block and Heavy Armor. Protect nearby friends. No way to force enemies to attack you.
Death knightA lone heavy warriorTwo-handed and Heavy Armor. One summoned helper. Restoration heals you.
Stealth rangerSneak and shoot everythingArchery and Sneak. Scout first. Keep shooting when spotted. Our archery patch removes critical bonuses while you sneak.
Gandalf-style wizardFragile on your ownDestruction attacks. Alteration protects. Restoration heals. Earn a staff later.
StorytellerTalk your way throughSpeech, Restoration and a weapon. Keep a damage option.
Blood mageDrain Health to survive aloneVampiric Bolt steals Health at range. Vampiric Touch drains nearby enemies.
Undead hunterFight undead with weaponsSunbeam burns undead. Restoration also heals friends. Carry a weapon.
Battlefield supporterBoost your followersCourage strengthens nearby allies. Add Restoration and a weapon.
Skeleton archer commanderFight beside one summoned helperConjure Skeleton Archer supports your bow. One active summon per player. Never raise corpses.
Night scoutRely on racial night visionNight Eye helps you see. Sneak and Archery help you scout.
Staff summonerUse a staff beside your summonStaff of the Atronach strengthens certain staff spells and summons. One active summon per player. Potent atronachs and thralls scale with your level.
Necromancer-healerRaise the deadRestoration first, Conjuration second. Summon one helper. Never raise corpses.
HealerHeal yourself and your followerRestoration heals friends. Alteration protects you. Keep a weapon or attack spell.
PaladinSword, shield, heal yourselfOne-handed, Block and Restoration. Block enemies, then switch to healing.
