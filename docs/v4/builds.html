Quick answer

QuestionShort answer
What should I play?Anything you enjoy. For ideas, see Build sheets and the Party ledger.

Builds, before and after

BuildBefore: single playerAfter: our party
HealerHeal yourself and your followerRestoration first, Alteration for protection. Keep friends standing, and keep a weapon or spell for yourself.
PaladinSword, shield, heal yourselfOne-handed, Block and Restoration. Switch between holding the line and emergency heals.
Necromancer-healerRaise the deadRestoration first, Conjuration second. Summon one helper; never raise corpses.
Death knightA lone heavy warriorTwo-handed and Heavy Armor up front, one summoned helper, enough Restoration for yourself.
Two-handed guardianProtect yourself and your followerTwo-handed, Block and Heavy Armor. Stand between enemies and friends; there's no taunt, so stay close.
Stealth rangerSneak and shoot everythingArchery and Sneak find the opening. Keep moving and shooting after you're spotted.
Gandalf-style wizardFragile on your ownDestruction and Alteration for fire and protection, backup Restoration. Earn a staff later.
StorytellerTalk your way throughSpeech, Restoration and a weapon. Keep a reliable damage fallback.
