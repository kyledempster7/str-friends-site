Quarantined perks and powers

Page 3 of 3: Skills · Races · Quarantined perks and powers Next topic: Spells and magic →

Listen to this section: Episode 3, Playing as a party · Episode 8, The magic path, part two: healing and summoning (opens in a new tab)(each opens the Audio guide in a new tab)

Quarantined means don't take, cast or use it until the host announces that its co-op test passed. These are agreed campaign rules. The audio guide says “held” for the same thing. Perks our Friend Kit patches changed are not on this list: they are fine to take, but nobody has played them together yet. Highborn and Adrenaline Rush, which the Friend Kit race patch gives back in revision 7, are not quarantined either. A quarantine does not mean a crash is proven. Spells are on the Spells page. Can I use this? lists everything.
Perk or powerSingle playerCo-op
Red Sand Dance (Redguard)For 45 seconds, sprinting slows time by 90%.Quarantined (don't use yet): this racial power.
Contingency (High Elf)Stores equipped spells. Casts them free when your chosen condition occurs.Quarantined: automatic spell casting through this power. Don't use yet.
Beast Tongue (Wood Elf)Turns the nearest carnivorous animal into an ally. Allows one animal companion.Quarantined (don't use yet): this animal-recruiting power.
Spirit Walk (Dark Elf)Spends two Spirit Favor. Summons two invulnerable ancestors for 45 seconds.Quarantined even with no other summon. Don't use yet.
Extra active summonsBetter Vanilla Perks lets Summoner, Puppeteer, Twin Souls and Dragonborn rank 2 add summon slots. In our game the held-perk patch removes every extra slot.Keep one active summon per player. One summon is our starting test budget, not an engine limit.
Dark Souls 2 (Conjuration perk)Reanimated bodies can explode in flames when combat begins.Quarantined (don't use yet): the perk and its corpse explosions. The held-perk patch locks it, so it shows greyed out.
Necromage (Restoration 70)Needs Resurgence. Gives a 30% bonus against undead.Quarantined (don't use yet): the held-perk patch locks it, so it shows greyed out. Taking Resurgence does not unlock it.
Time-slowing effectsOur patches removed the slowdowns from Strong Reflexes rank 2 and Steady Hand. Red Sand Dance still slows time for you.Quarantined (don't use yet) for group pacing and testing. Skyrim Together can sync slow time. We don't claim all slow time breaks sync. Skyrim Together compatibility explanation
