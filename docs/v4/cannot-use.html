Chapter 02 · Skills and races

Perks and powers on hold

Perks and powers on hold, before and after

Held until we test them together. Spell holds are in chapter 03, and everything is on Can I use this?
Perk or powerBefore: single playerAfter: our rule
Strong Reflexes rank 2 (Block 50)Halves damage while blocking and slows time when blocking enemy power attacks with melee gear.Hold rank 2. Rank 1's shield protection at Block 30 is allowed.
Steady Hand, whole branch (from Archery 40)Rank 1 slows time while zooming and adds critical chance; rank 2 adds critical chance and protection against disarming.Hold both ranks. Rank 2 does not clear the first rank's slowdown.
Red Sand Dance (Redguard)For 45 seconds, sprinting slows time by 90%.Hold this racial power.
Contingency (High Elf)Stores equipped spells and casts them free when your chosen condition occurs.Hold automatic spell casting through this racial power.
Beast Tongue (Wood Elf)Turns the nearest carnivorous animal into an ally; allows one animal companion.Hold this animal-recruiting power.
Spirit Walk (Dark Elf)Spends two Spirit Favor to summon two invulnerable ancestors for 45 seconds.Hold this power, even if you have no other summon.
Extra active summonsBVP's Summoner, Puppeteer, Twin Souls and Dragonborn rank 2 increase how many helpers you can control.Keep one active summon per player, regardless of unlocked capacity. Held summons and corpse raising remain excluded.
Dark Souls 2 (Conjuration perk)Reanimated bodies can explode in flames when combat begins.Hold the perk and its corpse explosions.
Dragonborn rank 4: spectral drum (Speech 60)Grants a summoned spectral drum that restores followers' Stamina, alongside better trading prices.Hold the drum power. Its effect on other players (not verified).
Dragonborn rank 5: Dremora Merchant (Speech 80)Grants a summonable Dremora to buy and sell goods, alongside better trading prices.Hold the merchant power.
Torch auto-unlock (Lockpicking)Novice through Master Locks can open matching locks while you hold a torch outside combat.Hold automatic unlocking. Use lockpicks manually.
Resurgence (Restoration)Strengthens Restoration spells by 30%Hold until the group's first healer test.
