Quarantined perks and powers

Quarantined: don't use these until we test them together. Check Spells for spell restrictions. Can I use this? lists everything.
Perk or powerSingle playerCo-op
Strong Reflexes rank 2 (Block 50)Halves blocking damage. Melee blocks against power attacks slow time.Quarantined (don't use yet): rank 2. Rank 1's shield protection at Block 30 is allowed.
Steady Hand, whole branch (from Archery 40)Rank 1 slows time while zooming and adds critical chance. Rank 2 adds critical chance and protection against disarming.Quarantined (don't use yet): both ranks. Rank 2 does not clear the first rank's slowdown.
Red Sand Dance (Redguard)For 45 seconds, sprinting slows time by 90%.Quarantined (don't use yet): this racial power.
Contingency (High Elf)Stores equipped spells. Casts them free when your chosen condition occurs.Quarantined: automatic spell casting through this power. Don't use yet.
Beast Tongue (Wood Elf)Turns the nearest carnivorous animal into an ally. Allows one animal companion.Quarantined (don't use yet): this animal-recruiting power.
Spirit Walk (Dark Elf)Spends two Spirit Favor. Summons two invulnerable ancestors for 45 seconds.Quarantined even with no other summon. Don't use yet.
Extra active summonsSummoner, Puppeteer, Twin Souls, Dragonborn rank 2 increase summon capacity.Keep one active summon per player, regardless of unlocked capacity. Quarantined summons and corpse raising remain excluded.
Dark Souls 2 (Conjuration perk)Reanimated bodies can explode in flames when combat begins.Quarantined (don't use yet): the perk and its corpse explosions.
Dragonborn rank 4: spectral drum (Speech 60)Summons a spectral drum that restores followers' Stamina. Improves prices.Quarantined (don't use yet): the drum power. Its effect on other players (not verified).
Dragonborn rank 5: Dremora Merchant (Speech 80)Summons a Dremora to buy and sell goods. Improves prices.Quarantined (don't use yet): the merchant power.
Torch auto-unlock (Lockpicking)Novice through Master Locks open matching locks outside combat. Requires a torch.Quarantined (don't use yet): automatic unlocking. Use lockpicks manually.
Resurgence (Restoration)Strengthens Restoration spells by 30%Quarantined (don't use yet) until the group's first healer test.
