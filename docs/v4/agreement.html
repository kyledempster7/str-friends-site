Proposed party agreement, not the game's rules

TopicSingle playerCo-op
Your stuffAll yoursYour gear and gold. No spawning, duplicating or reload-farming.
UpgradesKeep themOffer upgrades to whoever needs them. Alternate ties. No leader bonus.
Quest itemsYou take themThe leader handles quest people and items.
Shared costsNot a thingVoluntary. A pledge is not a payment.
SuppliesYour bagName who holds shared supplies. Test personal storage first.
HomesBuy whatever you likeEarn them. Agree who buys and who visits.
Absent friendsNot a thingProtect their belongings and their story.
