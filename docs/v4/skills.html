02 · Skills and races

Skills

Page 1 of 3: Skills · Races · Quarantined perks and powers Next: Races →

Listen to this section: Episode 6, The combat path: blades, shields and armor · Episode 7, The magic path, part one: attack and control · Episode 9, The stealth path: sneak, bow and silver tongue · Episode 12, What to go looking for (opens in a new tab)(each opens the Audio guide in a new tab)

Vanilla means the unmodded game. Mods describes our game: Better Vanilla Perks (part of revision 7) with the held-perk and Quiet Hunter patches from the Friend Kit. Perks the patches changed end with “(changed for co-op)” in the perk menu. Nobody has played them together yet.
SkillVanillaModsOur rule
AlchemyAlchemist strengthens crafted potions and poisons. Snakeblood grants poison resistance.Alchemist adds poison damage to weapon attacks. Snakeblood adds waterbreathing. Poisoner makes melee poison doses last longer.No extra rule
AlterationNovice through Master perks reduce spell costs. Magic Resistance provides separate magic defense.Novice adds poison resistance. Apprentice: frost. Adept: fire. Expert: shock. Master: disease.No extra rule
ArcheryOverdraw raises bow damage. Power Shot adds stagger. Steady Hand slows time while zooming.Overdraw adds armor penetration and longer arrow poisons. Power Shot adds penetration. Bullseye adds critical damage and disarming. Steady Hand rank 1 adds 10% bow critical chance and 5% more bow damage, with no time slowdown. Overdraw ranks 2 to 5 cost 5, 10, 15 and 20% of your melee and unarmed damage. Critical Shot and Bullseye give no critical bonus while you sneak.Pick one of each pair: Steady Hand or Quick Draw, Power Shot or Ranger. The Quiet Hunter patch greys out the other.
BlockShield Wall improves blocking. Quick Reflexes slows time while blocking a power attack.Shield Wall becomes Iron Will, adding unarmed damage. Strong Reflexes rank 1 halves power-attack damage when shield-blocking. Rank 2 halves all damage while you block; our held-perk patch removes its slowdown.No extra rule
ConjurationSummoner increases casting distance. Twin Souls allows two summons. Elemental Potency strengthens atronachs. An atronach is a summoned elemental spirit.Summoner and Puppeteer cast summons twice and three times as far away. Twin Souls makes Conjuration spells cost 10% less magicka. Our held-perk patch removes the extra summon slots Better Vanilla Perks would add. Staff of the Atronach strengthens certain spells. Potent atronachs and thralls scale with your level.One active summon per player. Quarantined (don't use yet): corpse raising and Dark Souls 2, which is locked and greyed out.
DestructionAugmented Flames, Frost and Shock add equal damage bonuses. Rune Master increases placement range.Augmented Flames/Frost/Shock rank 1: 20/30/40% more damage, cheaper spells. Rune Master also doubles rune capacity.No extra rule
EnchantingEnchanter strengthens new enchantments. Soul Siphon recharges weapons from creature kills.Enchanter also recharges weapons from nearby kills. Corpus Enchanter strengthens additional enchantments. Soul Siphon stacks with Enchanter.No extra rule
Heavy ArmorJuggernaut raises armor rating. Well Fitted requires head, chest, hands and feet armored.Juggernaut adds material-specific arrow, stagger or magic protection. Well Fitted and Matching Set require three pieces. Choose any helmet.No extra rule
IllusionNovice Illusion reduces spell cost. Quiet Casting requires the Animage and Kindred Mage path.Novice Illusion adds arrow avoidance. Quiet Casting: 50 Illusion or Sneak, no earlier perks. Only works while sneaking.Enemy-control spells do not control players.
Light ArmorAgile Defender raises armor rating. Custom Fit requires head, chest, hands and feet armored.Agile Defender adds material-specific bonuses: cheaper power attacks, stagger resistance. Custom Fit and Matching Set need only three pieces.No extra rule
LockpickingNovice through Master Locks make matching locks easier to pick.Novice through Master Locks make matching locks easier to pick. Quick Hands no longer needs earlier perks. Our held-perk patch removes Better Vanilla Perks' torch unlock, so you pick every lock by hand.No extra rule
One-HandedArmsman raises weapon damage. Bladesman adds sword critical hits. Hack and Slash makes axes bleed targets.Armsman also adds critical chance. Bladesman adds bleeding. Hack and Slash boosts axe power attacks. Flurry speeds main-hand attacks.No extra rule
PickpocketLight Fingers improves theft chances. Extra Pockets adds 100 carrying capacity.Light Fingers also improves arrow recovery. Extra Pockets gains additional ranks, reaching 300 extra carrying capacity.Coordinate theft and shared loot.
RestorationRegeneration strengthens healing by 50%. Recovery improves Magicka regeneration.Regeneration becomes Resurgence. In our game it makes Restoration spells cost 10% less magicka; Better Vanilla Perks alone makes them 30% stronger. Mastery perks add Magicka regeneration. Recovery also improves Stamina regeneration.Quarantined (don't use yet): Necromage (Restoration 70). It is locked and shows greyed out.
SmithingSteel Smithing improves steel equipment. Arcane Blacksmith enables tempering enchanted equipment.Basic Smithing also improves iron. Thalmor Smithing adds Falmer crafting. Arcane Blacksmith improves enchanted gear, leather, hide and faction armor.No extra rule
SneakBackstab boosts one-handed sneak attacks. Shadow Warrior briefly breaks detection when crouching in combat.Backstab extends bonuses to melee and spell attacks. Shadow Warrior adds night vision and greatsword sneak damage. Its second rank adds combat invisibility.No extra rule
SpeechHaggling improves prices. Intimidation improves threatening dialogue.Dragonborn replaces Haggling: better prices (rank 2: 15%, rank 4: 25%, rank 5: 30%), skill-books and steam. Better Vanilla Perks also adds an extra summon, a drum and a Dremora merchant; our held-perk patch removes all three. Intimidation also grants Battlecry.No extra rule
Two-HandedBarbarian raises damage. Limbsplitter makes battleaxes bleed targets. Skullcrusher ignores armor.Barbarian adds armor penetration and greatsword bleeding. Limbsplitter boosts axe power attacks. Skullcrusher adds damage against undead.No extra rule

Sources: Better Vanilla Perks
