Chapter 02 · Skills and races

Skills

Skills, before and after

Better Vanilla Perks is part of our collection (revision 6).
SkillBefore: vanillaAfter: Better Vanilla PerksOur rule
AlchemyAlchemist strengthens crafted potions and poisons; Snakeblood grants poison resistance.Alchemist adds poison damage to weapon attacks; Snakeblood adds waterbreathing; Poisoner makes melee poison doses last longer.—
AlterationNovice through Master perks reduce spell costs; Magic Resistance provides separate magic defense.Novice Alteration adds poison resistance; Apprentice adds frost resistance, Adept fire, Expert shock, and Master disease resistance.—
ArcheryOverdraw raises bow damage; Power Shot adds stagger; Steady Hand slows time while zooming.Overdraw adds armor penetration and longer arrow poisons; Power Shot adds penetration; Bullseye adds critical damage and disarming.Quarantined (don't use yet): the whole Steady Hand branch, starting at Archery 40.
BlockShield Wall improves blocking; Quick Reflexes slows time while blocking a power attack.Shield Wall becomes Iron Will, adding unarmed damage. Strong Reflexes rank 1 halves power-attack damage when shield-blocking; rank 2 adds slowdown.Quarantined (don't use yet): Strong Reflexes rank 2, Block 50. Rank 1, Block 30, is allowed.
ConjurationSummoner increases casting distance; Twin Souls allows two summons; Elemental Potency strengthens atronachs.Summoner and Puppeteer add summon slots; Twin Souls adds two. Staff of the Atronach strengthens certain spells and makes atronachs scale.One active summon per player. Quarantined (don't use yet): corpse raising and Dark Souls 2 explosions.
DestructionAugmented Flames, Frost and Shock add equal damage bonuses; Rune Master increases placement range.Augmented Flames/Frost/Shock rank 1 adds 20/30/40% damage and reduces spell costs; Rune Master also doubles rune capacity.—
EnchantingEnchanter strengthens new enchantments; Soul Siphon recharges weapons from creature kills.Enchanter also recharges weapons from nearby kills; Corpus Enchanter strengthens additional enchantments; Soul Siphon stacks with Enchanter.—
Heavy ArmorJuggernaut raises armor rating; Well Fitted needs heavy armor on head, chest, hands and feet.Juggernaut adds material-specific arrow, stagger or magic protection. Well Fitted and Matching Set need three pieces, freeing your helmet choice.—
IllusionNovice Illusion reduces spell cost; Quiet Casting requires the Animage and Kindred Mage path.Novice Illusion adds arrow avoidance. Quiet Casting needs 50 Illusion or Sneak, with no earlier perks, and works while sneaking.Enemy-control spells do not control players.
Light ArmorAgile Defender raises armor rating; Custom Fit requires light armor on head, chest, hands and feet.Agile Defender adds material-specific benefits, including cheaper power attacks or stagger resistance. Custom Fit and Matching Set need only three pieces.—
LockpickingNovice through Master Locks make their lock difficulties easier to pick.Novice through Master Locks also bypass matching locks with a torch outside combat. Quick Hands no longer needs earlier perks.Quarantined (don't use yet): torch auto-unlock. Use lockpicks manually.
One-HandedArmsman raises weapon damage; Bladesman adds sword critical hits; Hack and Slash makes axes bleed targets.Armsman also adds critical chance; Bladesman adds bleeding; Hack and Slash boosts axe power attacks. Flurry speeds main-hand attacks.—
PickpocketLight Fingers improves theft chances; Extra Pockets adds 100 carrying capacity.Light Fingers also improves arrow recovery; Extra Pockets gains additional ranks, reaching 300 extra carrying capacity.Coordinate theft and shared loot.
RestorationRegeneration strengthens healing by 50%; Recovery improves Magicka regeneration.Regeneration becomes Resurgence, strengthening Restoration by 30%. Mastery perks add Magicka regeneration; Recovery also improves Stamina regeneration.Quarantined (don't use yet): Resurgence until the group's first healer test.
SmithingSteel Smithing improves steel equipment; Arcane Blacksmith enables tempering enchanted equipment.Basic Smithing also improves iron; Thalmor Smithing adds Falmer crafting; Arcane Blacksmith improves enchanted gear, leather, hide and faction armor.—
SneakBackstab boosts one-handed sneak attacks; Shadow Warrior briefly breaks detection when crouching in combat.Backstab extends bonuses to melee and spell attacks. Shadow Warrior adds night vision and greatsword sneak damage; its second rank adds combat invisibility.—
SpeechHaggling improves prices; Intimidation improves threatening dialogue.Haggling becomes Dragonborn: better prices plus skill-book, summon, steam, drum and merchant benefits. Intimidation also grants Battlecry.Quarantined (don't use yet): Dragonborn rank 4's drum and rank 5's merchant. Rank 2 does not increase our one-summon limit.
Two-HandedBarbarian raises damage; Limbsplitter makes battleaxes bleed targets; Skullcrusher ignores armor.Barbarian adds armor penetration and greatsword bleeding; Limbsplitter boosts axe power attacks; Skullcrusher adds damage against undead.—

Sources: Better Vanilla Perks
