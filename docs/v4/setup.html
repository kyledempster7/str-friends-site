Chapter 04 · A night of adventure

First-time setup

Setting up the first time, before and after

Do this once. The full steps and installer choices are on our Nexus collection page.
StepBefore: single playerAfter: our co-op
Get the gameSkyrim Special Edition on SteamThe same. You don't need the Anniversary upgrade.
Prepare the gameNothing to doRun the Friend Kit from our Nexus page once. It moves four built-in add-ons aside. Never use Steam's "Verify integrity" afterwards.
Install modsOptional, one at a timeInstall our whole collection with Vortex from our Nexus page, picking the options it lists
Check two switchesNot neededIn Vortex, make sure SkyrimTogether.esp and SkyrimTogetherQuestPatches.esp are turned on.
Start the gameSteam's Play buttonLaunch Skyrim Together (SkyrimTogether.exe). Steam's Play button skips the mods.
Make your characterPlay through HelgenFinish Helgen Express offline, then save outside before you ever connect.

Sources: Our collection on Nexus
