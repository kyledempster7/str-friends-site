How to get Glamdring

A friend without Weapon Collection LotR will not see your Glamdring, and you will not see theirs. Handing one to a player without the mod has not been tested.
StepWhat to doOur rule or note
Install the modWeapon Collection LotR adds 26 craftable weapons. Glamdring is one of them.It is optional. Everyone who should see your sword needs it too.
Pick the right oneThe mod has two Glamdrings: a one-handed sword and a two-handed sword. Take the one-handed Glamdring. The two-handed one fills both hands and leaves no room for the staff.Check the weapon type in the crafting list before you spend materials.
Craft itIt is a craftable weapon. Smithing makes it better, as with any sword.Where it sits in the crafting menu is not checked in the game yet.
Put it in your right handOpen Favorites, choose the sword for the right hand and the staff for the left. Do it before a fight.Menus do not pause.

Staffs for a wizard with a sword

StaffUse it?Why
Staff of Firebolts, Staff of Fireballs, Staff of Flames, Staff of Lightning BoltsBest picksMysticism adds these. They are plain Destruction staffs: a bolt, a blast or a stream. They need no summon and no corpse.
Frost, shock and light staffs of the same kindGood picksSame idea, other damage types. Pick the type that matches the Destruction perks you took.
Summon staffs, such as the Sanguine Rose and the Staff of the FamiliarOne at a timeSkyrim Together used to spawn two creatures from these two staffs. That is fixed. Other summon staffs were not checked. Do not summon again while one is out.
Staff of the Zombie, Staff of Reanimation, Staff of Revenant, Staff of the Dread Zombie, Staff of Dead ThrallAvoidThey raise corpses. Our party rule is never to raise corpses.
Fear and frenzy staffsAvoidFear-type spells cannot be used on other players. They can still hit townspeople.

Your High Elf powers

PowerWhat it doesOur rule or note
Highborn (power)Restores Magicka quickly for 60 seconds, once a day. Press it at the start of a hard fight.From the Friend Kit race patch. Tested alone: 90 Magicka came back in about 3 seconds instead of about 10. Not tested with two players.
Elven Supremacy (always on)Skills at 100 are 10% more effective.No extra rule
Gold and Glass (always on)Magicka and Stamina recover faster when they fall below half. The price is up to 100 less armor and 50% less magic resistance.Stay behind the front line. Armor and magic resistance drop when you are low.
Shimmering Threads (always on)Weapons and armor are 15% weaker. Enchantments are 15% stronger.Your staff is an enchantment, so it gains. Your Glamdring loses a little.
Living Arcana (always on)Our notes name it as a combat effect.What it does is not checked yet. Do not count on it.
Contingency (power)Stores equipped spells and casts them free when a condition you choose occurs.Not yet. Held for co-op. Do not use it. The race patch does not remove it from your list.

Fighting with a staff and a sword

TipWhat to doOur rule or note
Perks for the swordTake Armsman first, then Bladesman. Armsman makes one-handed weapons do 20% more damage at rank 1 and 100% more at rank 5. Bladesman adds critical chance and bleeding to swords.From Better Vanilla Perks. The held-perk patch leaves both open.
Perks for the staffTake the Destruction perks for the damage type of your staff. Staff of the Atronach strengthens summoned atronachs.Leave Staff of the Atronach until the group is happy with one summon.
Lead with the staffShoot from range. Draw Glamdring when an enemy reaches you.Shimmering Threads makes the staff stronger than the sword. This is our suggestion, not a tested rule.
Use Highborn earlyPress it when a hard fight starts, so staff and spells keep flowing for the full minute.Once a day.
Carry soul gemsCarry filled soul gems. Recharge the staff between fights at an Arcane Enchanter.The staff's charge count is not named as synced. Watch your own charge.
Target lockThe target lock in True Directional Movement keeps an enemy in view while you cast from the staff and swing.Both mods are installed. Not tested with two players.
No blockingYou cannot block with a staff in one hand and a sword in the other. Keep moving, and save Stamina so you can sprint out of danger.Precision swings cost Stamina. Stand behind DK.
Set favorites firstPut the sword and the staff in Favorites before you go in.Menus do not pause.
Your spells and arrowsA bolt from you will not hurt a friend. It will hurt a guard.Players cannot hurt players in our setup. Friendly non-player characters can still be hit.
Sword swingsPrecision and True Directional Movement give real hitboxes. On a friend's screen your swing may look late, even when the hit counts.Remote melee is not proved in our notes.
