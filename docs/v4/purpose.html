08 · Our purpose

Our purpose

Page 1 of 2: The six orders · Vote Next: Vote →

Pick one purpose for the whole campaign. It gives our walks a reason. Read all six, then vote.

These are ideas. The house rules are suggestions, not server rules.

Our quarantined list and one summon each still apply.

The six orders

Everything here uses the base game, its add-ons and our installed mods.
OrderKindThe idea
Relic WardensGuardingCarry cursed relics to one place. Seal them there.
Free CompanyFighting for payA band of sellswords. Take jobs and bounties from the holds.
Trading CompanyTradeA trading house. Carry goods between holds and guard caravans.
Antiquarians and Map-makersLearningScholars on foot. Explore every ruin and collect lore books.
Monster HuntersHuntingHunters of vampires, necromancers and dragons. Dawnguard work.
Road PilgrimsFaithFaithful travellers. Visit Divine shrines and help people on the road.

Relic Wardens

PartIn this order
Long goalFind cursed relics: Dragon Priest masks, Daedric artifacts, necromancer tomes. Seal every one in a place we choose.
House rule 1Walk everywhere. No fast travel.
House rule 2Relics are never sold or traded.
House rule 3Choose the sealing place first. Seal every relic there.
Stealth archerScouts the route. Shoots tomb guards before the party enters.
WizardStudies each relic. Fights with direct fire, frost and shock.
Sword and shieldGuards the relic bearer in narrow tomb halls.
Healer: cleric or paladinKeeps the bearer alive. Sunbeam burns undead guards.

Free Company

PartIn this order
Long goalEarn a name in all nine holds. Finish one job in each.
House rule 1Walk between holds. No fast travel.
House rule 2Share job pay evenly, four ways.
House rule 3Finish one job before taking the next.
Stealth archerOpens fights from range. Takes quiet kills on bounties.
WizardHits groups with direct spells. Calm and Fear handle the rest.
Sword and shieldHolds the front line. Takes the first hit.
Healer: cleric or paladinKeeps the company standing between fights.

Trading Company

PartIn this order
Long goalRun trade routes through all nine holds. Count every delivery.
House rule 1Walk the trade routes. No fast travel.
House rule 2Buy goods in one hold. Sell them in another.
House rule 3Guard every cargo. Never leave a caravan behind.
Stealth archerScouts the road ahead. Picks off bandits from cover.
WizardGuards the caravan with direct spells. Fear scatters raiders.
Sword and shieldWalks beside the wagon. Blocks arrows meant for the cargo.
Healer: cleric or paladinHeals travellers on the road. Heal Other works on followers too.

Antiquarians and Map-makers

PartIn this order
Long goalFill the map and the library. Visit every ruin. Read every lore book.
House rule 1Walk everywhere. No fast travel.
House rule 2Describe each new place in party chat.
House rule 3Use Atlas Map Markers and moreHUD to track finds.
Stealth archerScouts ruins quietly. Finds the safe way in.
WizardReads each book first. Night Eye lights dark ruins.
Sword and shieldLeads the way through traps and draugr halls.
Healer: cleric or paladinHeals trap wounds. Keeps the party's book list.

Monster Hunters

PartIn this order
Long goalKeep a hunter's tally. Clear a vampire lair, a necromancer lair and a dragon. Random dragon attacks are off in our game, so dragons come through quests.
House rule 1Walk to every hunt. No fast travel.
House rule 2Hunt as a group. Nobody chases a target alone.
House rule 3Never raise corpses. One summon each.
Stealth archerShoots from the dark. Scouts each lair first.
WizardFire works well on vampires. Direct spells hit from afar.
Sword and shieldHolds the lair door. Blocks the big hits.
Healer: cleric or paladinSunbeam burns undead. See the Vigilant of Stendarr build.

Road Pilgrims

PartIn this order
Long goalDo one good deed in each of the nine holds. Then cross to Solstheim.
House rule 1Walk everywhere. No fast travel.
House rule 2Pray at every Divine shrine you pass.
House rule 3Take no pay for a good deed.
Stealth archerHunts food for the camp. Keeps watch at night.
WizardGuards pilgrims with direct spells. Calm ends a fight kindly.
Sword and shieldEscorts the group. Stands between pilgrims and bandits.
Healer: cleric or paladinThe heart of the order. Heal Other helps strangers on the road.
