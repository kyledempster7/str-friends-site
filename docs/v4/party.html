Group play

Group play, before and after

SituationBefore: single playerAfter: our party
ChasedRun or fightSay "On me". Move toward the group; the frontliner steps in.
HurtDrink a potionSay "Healing here" and come within reach. Healing is not resurrection.
Dangerous casterDeal with it yourselfSay "Caster first" and focus it. Keep a retreat route open.
Ready to moveJust goType or say READY.
Need helpDrink a potion or runType HELP and your location.
Mod settingsYours aloneEveryone uses matching mods. Agree on changes together.
