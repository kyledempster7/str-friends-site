Group play, before and after

Group play, before and after

SituationBefore: single playerAfter: our party
ChasedRun or fightSay "On me". Move toward the group; the frontliner steps in.
HurtDrink a potionSay "Healing here" and come within reach. Healing is not resurrection.
Dangerous casterDeal with it yourselfSay "Caster first" and focus it. Keep a retreat route open.
Ready to moveJust goType or say READY.
Need helpReloadType HELP and your location.
Mod settingsYours aloneEveryone uses matching mods. Agree on changes together.

The party leader

WhoDoes
The leader (one for the whole playthrough)Talks to quest people, handles scenes and quest items, enters places first.
EveryoneFights bosses, clears dungeons, solves puzzles.
A different leaderMeans a separate campaign with separate characters.

Chat codes

TypeMeans
READYReady to go.
HOLDFind safety and wait.
HELPHelp now; add your location.
BRBTaking a break.
/party or /localMessage only the party, or only nearby players. F2 or Right Ctrl opens chat.
