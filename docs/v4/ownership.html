07 · What belongs to you

Ownership

Page 1 of 2: Ownership · Our agreement (proposed) Next: Our agreement (proposed) →

Listen to this section: Episode 10, Inventory, looting and a home (opens in a new tab)(each opens the Audio guide in a new tab)

Quick answer

QuestionShort answer
Where do I keep my things?Use your pack until you own a home. Test home chests with a cheap item first.
ThingSingle playerCo-op
Before you own a homeUse your pack or a container that never resets.Keep valuables in your pack. Containers you don't own can reset.
Carried inventoryGear and supplies travel with your character and save.Each player carries their own items. Equipment and inventories synchronize.
World and chest lootYou collect the world's loot for yourself.Ordinary chests and pre-placed loot are shared. Agree who takes upgrades.
GoldYou hold and spend your own gold.Your gold stays yours. No death gold penalty. Death may also pay your crime bounties. Not tested here.
Private home chestsOwned homes provide personal storage. Choose a safe container.Chests inside the city homes you can buy (such as Breezehome) and inside Elysium Estate belong to that player alone. Other players do not see them. Hearthfire houses may differ. Not tested with two players: test your chest with cheap items first.
Shared storage and tradingContainers store items. Some containers reset and are unsafe for lasting storage.No shared chest keeps items for long. A chest outside a home is shared only while everyone is in the same area. When the last player leaves, the server forgets what is in it, and the next player in can overwrite it. Use it only while all of you are present, and try cheap items first. Crafted potions and enchanted gear may not reach another player (reported by other players; not tested here).
Dropped itemsYou can drop an item and pick it up again.Dropped items aren't shared on our server. Trade through a tested shared chest.
Quest itemsTake quest items like the Golden Claw. Advance quests yourself.The party leader takes quest items and handles quest dialogue.
Buying a home as a non-leaderMeet the city's requirements, then pay its steward.The leader handles prerequisite quests. Non-leader payment, keys and ownership remain unverified. Another player's purchase may not buy you a home.
Hearthfire building and furnishingBuy a plot, gather materials and build your own house.Vanilla/DLC interiors are personal. Building, steward purchases and party visits remain unverified.
Our group houseBuy or earn homes across Skyrim.Elysium Estate, southwest of Whiterun, is our group house. Its Nexus page says a quest unlocks it. We have not tested how you get in, or two players inside it. Chests inside it are private to each player.
