Chapter 07 · What belongs to you

Ownership

Quick answer

QuestionShort answer
Where do I keep my things?In your pack until you have a home. Then in your own home's chests, after testing one with a cheap item.

Ownership, before and after

ThingBefore: single playerAfter: our co-op
Before you own a homeKeep valuables in your pack or in a container that doesn't resetKeep valuables in your pack. Containers you don't own can reset.
Carried inventoryGear and supplies travel with your character and save.Each player carries their own items; equipped gear and inventories synchronize.
World and chest lootYou collect the world's loot for yourself.Ordinary chests and pre-placed loot are shared. Agree who takes upgrades.
GoldYou hold and spend your own gold.Personal purses; no death gold penalty in our setup. Crime bounties still cost gold.
Private home chestsOwned homes provide personal storage; choose a safe container.Vanilla/DLC home chests are unsynced. Test your chosen chest with cheap items; persistence here (not verified).
Shared storage and tradingContainers store items; some containers reset and are unsafe for lasting storage.Shared chest transfers synchronize. Test a cheap transfer first; lasting storage and reconnect results (not verified).
Dropped itemsYou can drop an item and pick it up again.Dropped items aren't shared on our server. Hand things over through a tested shared chest.
Quest itemsYou take items such as the Golden Claw and advance the quest yourself.Only the party leader takes quest items and handles quest dialogue.
Buying a home as a non-leaderMeet the city's requirements, then pay its steward.The leader handles prerequisite quests. Non-leader payment, keys and ownership (not verified); do not assume another player's purchase buys you a home.
Hearthfire building and furnishingBuy a plot, gather materials and build your own house.Vanilla/DLC interiors are personal. Building, steward purchases and visits with this party (not verified).
