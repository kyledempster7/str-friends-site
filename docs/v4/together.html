Chapter 01 · A shared world

What changes in multiplayer

Quick answer

QuestionShort answer
What changes from single player?One shared world: one quest leader, nothing pauses, and Master difficulty for everyone. The chart below has the details.

What changes in multiplayer, before and after

TopicBefore: single playerAfter: our co-op
SavesOne save, reload any timeEveryone keeps their own save. The server holds no copy. Save often; never reload while connected.
QuestsYou talk to everyoneOne party leader for the whole playthrough handles quest people, scenes and quest items, and enters places first. Everyone fights bosses and solves puzzles.
StartingHelgen introFinish Helgen or Helgen Express offline, save outside, then connect.
MenusOpening a menu pauses the gameSkyrim Souls RE keeps most menus live, and nothing pauses for the party. Get out of danger first.
DyingReload a saveYou respawn nearby, or at the entrance indoors. No gold lost. Crime bounties still apply.
DifficultyYour choiceMaster for everyone, set by the server. Enemies do not scale with party size.
Hitting friendsNo other playersPlayers can't hurt players. Followers and townsfolk can still be hurt.
Lock-onNo target lock-on; you aim and turn yourself.True Directional Movement adds lock-on. Our setting targets hostiles only.
KillcamsOnOff.
ExperienceYou earn your own experienceWhen anyone gains combat experience, everyone gets the same amount in their own last-used combat skill. Crafting experience is not shared.
Other nightsOne saveA smaller-party night uses separate characters.
TalkingNo other playersVoice, or text chat: F2 or Right Ctrl, type, Enter. /party and /local work.
FollowersRecruit an NPC companion to fight and carry items.Official advice: avoid followers except for quests; they can wander away.
Waiting and sleepingAdvance the clock by choosing hours to wait or sleep.The server controls time. Waiting and sleeping cannot advance it.
Reconnecting after a crashRestart and load your latest save.Load your latest campaign save, reconnect, check the usual leader, and regroup before questing. Progress recovery in this setup (not verified).
