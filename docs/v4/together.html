What changes in multiplayer

TopicSingle playerCo-op
Saving your progressOne save, reload any timeEveryone keeps their own save. The server holds no copy. Save often. Never reload while connected.
Following quests togetherYou talk to everyoneKeep one party leader for the whole playthrough. They handle quest dialogue, scenes and items. They enter first. Everyone fights bosses and solves puzzles.
Finishing the introductionHelgen introFinish Helgen or Helgen Express offline, save outside, then connect.
Opening menusOpening a menu pauses the gameSkyrim Souls RE keeps most menus live. Nothing pauses for the party. Get out of danger first.
Dying and respawningReload a saveYou respawn nearby, or at the entrance indoors. No gold lost. Crime bounties still apply.
Choosing difficultyYour choiceMaster for everyone, set by the server. Enemies do not scale with party size.
Hitting friendsNo other playersPlayers can't hurt players. Followers and townsfolk can still be hurt.
Locking onto enemiesNo target lock-on. You aim and turn yourself.True Directional Movement adds lock-on. Our setting targets hostiles only.
Watching kill animationsOnOff.
Earning skill experienceYou earn your own experienceWhen someone earns combat experience, everyone gets that amount. Each player's last-used combat skill gains it. Crafting experience isn't shared.
Playing with fewer friendsOne saveA smaller-party night uses separate characters.
Talking to friendsNo other playersPress F2 or Right Ctrl. Type, press Enter. /party, /local and voice work.
Recruiting followersRecruit an NPC companion to fight and carry items.Official advice: avoid followers except for quests. They can wander.
Waiting and sleepingAdvance the clock by choosing hours to wait or sleep.The server controls time. Waiting and sleeping cannot advance it.
Rejoining after a crashRestart and load your latest save.Load your latest campaign save. Reconnect. Check the usual leader. Regroup before questing. Progress recovery remains unverified.
