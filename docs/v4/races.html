Races

Imperious changes racial traits. Complete racial objectives to unlock powers. High Elf, Wood Elf, Dark Elf and Redguard have no racial power in our game.
RaceVanillaModsOur rule
High Elf+10 Illusion. +5 Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Enchanting, Restoration. +50 Magicka. Highborn rapidly restores Magicka for one minute.Level-100 skills improve. Low Magicka/Stamina recover faster but weaken defenses. Gear weakens, enchantments strengthen. Contingency stores spells for automatic casting.No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Contingency. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Argonian+10 Lockpicking. +5 Alteration, Light Armor, Pickpocket, Restoration, Sneak. Disease resistance, waterbreathing, stronger punches. Histskin rapidly restores Health.Faster swimming, waterbreathing, post-swim stealth/speed, low-resource recovery. Caustic Spit strips armor and magic resistance. Innate disease resistance is gone.No quarantined power. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Wood Elf+10 Archery. +5 Alchemy, Light Armor, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Sneak. Poison/disease resistance. Command Animal temporarily recruits an animal.Eat corpses for attributes. Harrier marks prey and weakens foes. Sense movement while still. Beast Tongue recruits animals. Loses innate poison/disease resistance.No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Beast Tongue. Harrier's effects for friends and racial unlocks (not verified).
Breton+10 Conjuration. +5 Alchemy, Alteration, Illusion, Restoration, Speech. 25% magic resistance, Conjure Familiar. Dragonskin temporarily absorbs hostile spells.15% magic resistance, low-Magicka absorption. Special standing-stone bonuses. Grail boosts recovery. Shared Ancestry borrows human or elven racial powers.Borrowed powers keep their quarantines. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Dark Elf+10 Destruction. +5 Alchemy, Alteration, Illusion, Light Armor, Sneak. 50% fire resistance, Sparks. Ancestor's Wrath burns nearby enemies.25% fire resistance. Spending Spirit Favor grants armor and further resistance. Whispers marks victims for rewards. Spirit Walk spends Favor to summon ancestors.No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Spirit Walk. Whispers rewards and racial unlocks in co-op (not verified).
Imperial+10 Restoration. +5 Block, Destruction, Enchanting, Heavy Armor, One-Handed. Imperial Luck finds gold. Voice of the Emperor calms nearby people.Discipline boosts nearby allies' armor/damage. Random resource regeneration in combat. Permanent attribute/resistance/skill tradeoffs. Colovian Star refills and temporarily boosts Magicka and Stamina.Discipline's benefit to players (not verified). Do not count on it as a party buff.
Khajiit+10 Sneak. +5 Alchemy, Archery, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, One-Handed. Stronger claws. Night Eye grants repeatable night vision.Faster movement. Sprinting costs extra Stamina. Quieter sneaking, Prowl leaps, stronger claw power attacks. Perception grants night vision and dampens sounds.No quarantined power. Co-op movement and racial unlocks (not verified).
Nord+10 Two-Handed. +5 Block, Light Armor, One-Handed, Smithing, Speech. 50% frost resistance. Battle Cry makes nearby enemies flee.25% frost resistance. Choose racial enemies for damage bonuses. Missing Health strengthens attacks/spells. Avatar restores Magicka/Stamina and adds frost resistance.No quarantined power. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Orc+10 Heavy Armor. +5 Block, Enchanting, One-Handed, Smithing, Two-Handed. Stronghold access. Berserker Rage doubles physical damage dealt and halves damage received.Bloodlust briefly boosts speed/recovery. Shockwave staggers. Gear strengthens but enchantments weaken. Berserk boosts attacks and reduces spell costs. May trigger randomly.No quarantined power. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Redguard+10 One-Handed. +5 Alteration, Archery, Block, Destruction, Smithing. 50% poison resistance. Adrenaline Rush rapidly restores Stamina.Disease resistance replaces poison resistance. Unlimited travel Stamina, recovery when injured, kill bonuses. Red Sand Dance slows time while sprinting.No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Red Sand Dance. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
