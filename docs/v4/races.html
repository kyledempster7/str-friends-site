02 · Skills and races

Races

Page 2 of 3: Skills · Races · Quarantined perks and powers Next: Quarantined perks and powers →

Listen to this section: Episode 5, Races and your racial power · Episode 12, What to go looking for (opens in a new tab)(each opens the Audio guide in a new tab)

Vanilla means the unmodded game. Imperious changes racial traits: Battle Cry (Nord), Imperial Luck and Voice of the Emperor (Imperial) appear only in the Vanilla column. Complete racial objectives to unlock powers. The Friend Kit race patch gives High Elves Highborn (faster Magicka regeneration) and Redguards Adrenaline Rush (faster Stamina regeneration) back, for a minute, once a day. Tested alone, not yet with two players. The Friend Kit also gives Wood Elves Hunter's Mark: 20 seconds of double bow damage that marks what your arrows hit, once a day. It is not tested in the game yet. Dark Elf has no racial power in our game. The Friend Kit also has a Five Races patch for Imperious. It stops the High Elf, Breton, Imperial, Nord and Wood Elf racial goals from being shared with the party, locks Contingency and Beast Tongue, stops Shared Ancestry borrowing held powers, and turns off NPCs casting Harrier, Avatar, Colovian Star, Spirit Walk and Ancestral Guardian on their own. Every player needs it, and it is not tested in the game yet. Do not use the Imperious menu option "Debug: manual": it still lets a player give themselves any power. Harrier, Whispers and racial progression still need testing from both sides. Prowl is a leap, not a verified teleport. Its co-op behavior is unverified. Don't count on Imperial Discipline as a party buff. Its armor and damage effects are not healing. NPCs can get racial abilities too, so avoiding a quarantined power doesn't prove all race behavior is tested.
