Chapter 02 · Skills and races

Races

Races, before and after

Imperious is part of our collection (revision 6). Its racial powers unlock through racial objectives, not at character creation.
RaceBefore: vanillaAfter: ImperiousOur rule
High Elf+10 Illusion; +5 Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Enchanting, Restoration; +50 Magicka. Highborn rapidly restores Magicka for one minute.Level-100 skills improve; low Magicka/Stamina recover faster but weaken defenses; gear weakens, enchantments strengthen. Contingency stores spells for automatic casting.Hold Contingency. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Argonian+10 Lockpicking; +5 Alteration, Light Armor, Pickpocket, Restoration, Sneak; disease resistance, waterbreathing, stronger punches. Histskin rapidly restores Health.Faster swimming, waterbreathing, post-swim stealth/speed, low-resource recovery. Caustic Spit strips armor and magic resistance. Innate disease resistance is gone.No held power; co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Wood Elf+10 Archery; +5 Alchemy, Light Armor, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Sneak; poison/disease resistance. Command Animal temporarily recruits an animal.Eat corpses for attributes; Harrier marks prey and weakens foes; sense movement while still. Beast Tongue recruits animals. Loses innate poison/disease resistance.Hold Beast Tongue. Harrier's effects for friends and racial unlocks (not verified).
Breton+10 Conjuration; +5 Alchemy, Alteration, Illusion, Restoration, Speech; 25% magic resistance, Conjure Familiar. Dragonskin temporarily absorbs hostile spells.15% magic resistance, low-Magicka absorption; special standing-stone bonuses; Grail boosts recovery. Shared Ancestry borrows human or elven racial powers.Borrowed powers keep their holds. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Dark Elf+10 Destruction; +5 Alchemy, Alteration, Illusion, Light Armor, Sneak; 50% fire resistance, Sparks. Ancestor's Wrath burns nearby enemies.25% fire resistance; spending Spirit Favor grants armor and further resistance. Whispers marks victims for rewards. Spirit Walk spends Favor to summon ancestors.Hold Spirit Walk. Whispers rewards and racial unlocks in co-op (not verified).
Imperial+10 Restoration; +5 Block, Destruction, Enchanting, Heavy Armor, One-Handed; Imperial Luck finds gold. Voice of the Emperor calms nearby people.Discipline boosts nearby allies' armor/damage; random resource regeneration in combat; permanent attribute/resistance/skill tradeoffs. Colovian Star refills and temporarily boosts Magicka and Stamina.Discipline's benefit to players (not verified); do not count on it as a party buff.
Khajiit+10 Sneak; +5 Alchemy, Archery, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, One-Handed; stronger claws. Night Eye grants repeatable night vision.Faster movement; sprinting costs extra Stamina. Quieter sneaking, Prowl leaps, stronger claw power attacks. Perception grants night vision and dampens sounds.No held power; co-op movement and racial unlocks (not verified).
Nord+10 Two-Handed; +5 Block, Light Armor, One-Handed, Smithing, Speech; 50% frost resistance. Battle Cry makes nearby enemies flee.25% frost resistance; choose racial enemies for damage bonuses; missing Health strengthens attacks/spells. Avatar restores Magicka/Stamina and adds frost resistance.No held power; co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Orc+10 Heavy Armor; +5 Block, Enchanting, One-Handed, Smithing, Two-Handed; stronghold access. Berserker Rage doubles physical damage dealt and halves damage received.Bloodlust briefly boosts speed/recovery; Shockwave staggers; gear strengthens but enchantments weaken. Berserk boosts attacks and reduces spell costs; may trigger randomly.No held power; co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).
Redguard+10 One-Handed; +5 Alteration, Archery, Block, Destruction, Smithing; 50% poison resistance. Adrenaline Rush rapidly restores Stamina.Disease resistance replaces poison resistance; unlimited travel Stamina, recovery when injured, kill bonuses. Red Sand Dance slows time while sprinting.Hold Red Sand Dance. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).

Sources: Imperious
