What we've checked

NameTypeStatusWhat to know
Corpse reanimationgame ruleQuarantined — don't use yetRaising an existing corpse makes it fight for you. Includes Raise Zombie, Reanimate Corpse, Revenant, Dread Zombie and Dead Thrall, plus corpse-raising staves and scrolls. Ordinary conjured helpers still have the one-summon limit. Source
More than one active summon per playergame ruleQuarantined — don't use yetQuarantined (don't use yet): extra helpers; keep one active summon per player. BVP perks add capacity, but our party limit stays one. Corpse raising, Beast Tongue, Spirit Walk and the quarantined Speech powers remain excluded. Source
Torch-assisted automatic unlocking — Better Vanilla Perksgame ruleQuarantined — don't use yetA torch can automatically open locks. Matching Lockpicking perks bypass locks while a torch is equipped outside combat. Pick locks manually in our campaign. Source
Ordinary creature summonsgame ruleConditionalSummon a creature to fight beside you; keep one active summon. Conjure Familiar keeps Mysticism's spell and wolf. Better Vanilla Perks supplies its text and look. Corpse raising and quarantined racial or Speech summons remain excluded. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Better Vanilla PerksmodAllowedReworks skill perks; use it with the group's named quarantines. Our revision 6 perk overhaul adds weapon, armor, crafting and magic bonuses. Exact effects with this party are not tested in co-op yet. Source
Imperious — Races of SkyrimmodAllowedGives each race distinct abilities and an unlockable power; four powers stay quarantined (don't use yet). Part of revision 6. Quarantined (don't use yet): Contingency, Red Sand Dance, Beast Tongue and Spirit Walk. NPCs gain racial abilities too; co-op effects and unlocks are untested. Source
Manual lockpickinggame ruleAllowedOpen locks with lockpicks. Opened locks synchronize. Torch-assisted automatic opening remains quarantined (don't use yet). Source
MysticismmodAllowedRebalances old spells and adds new ones; use the main package only. Better Vanilla Perks changes some spells and summon strength. Part of revision 6. Mark, Recall and corpse raising stay quarantined (don't use yet); one active summon each. Optional addons are outside our setup. Source
