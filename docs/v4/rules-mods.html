Mods and game rules

Revision 7 is the live collection. Our Nexus collection page has it.

What we've checked

NameTypeStatusWhat to know
Corpse reanimationgame ruleQuarantined — don't use yetRaising an existing corpse makes it fight for you. Includes Raise Zombie, Reanimate Corpse, Revenant, Dread Zombie and Dead Thrall, plus corpse-raising staves and scrolls. Ordinary conjured helpers still have the one-summon limit. Source
More than one active summon per playergame ruleConditionalKeep one active summon per player. Our perk patch removes the extra slots Better Vanilla Perks adds. Corpse raising, Beast Tongue and Spirit Walk stay excluded. Source
Ordinary creature summonsgame ruleConditionalSummon a creature to fight beside you; keep one active summon. Conjure Familiar keeps Mysticism's spell and wolf. Better Vanilla Perks supplies its text and look. Source
Better Vanilla PerksmodAllowedReworks skill perks. Two Friend Kit patches change some of them. Our revision 7 perk overhaul adds weapon, armor, crafting and magic bonuses. The patches remove slowed time, extra summon slots and torch unlocking, add the Archery choices, and lock Necromage and Dark Souls 2. Not tested with a full party yet. Source
Imperious — Races of SkyrimmodAllowedGives each race distinct abilities and an unlockable power; four powers stay quarantined (don't use yet). Part of revision 7. Quarantined (don't use yet): Contingency, Red Sand Dance, Beast Tongue and Spirit Walk. Our Friend Kit race patch gives High Elves Highborn, Redguards Adrenaline Rush and Wood Elves Hunter's Mark; Races has the details. NPCs gain racial abilities too; co-op effects and unlocks are untested. Source
Manual lockpickinggame ruleAllowedOpen locks with lockpicks. Opened locks synchronize. Our perk patch removes Better Vanilla Perks' torch unlock, so you pick every lock by hand. Source
MysticismmodAllowedRebalances old spells and adds new ones; use the main package only. Better Vanilla Perks changes some spells and summon strength. Part of revision 7. Mark, Recall and corpse raising stay quarantined (don't use yet); one active summon each. Optional addons are outside our setup. Source
