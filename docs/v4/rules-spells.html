Player healing is (not verified); use ordinary Restoration for friends. Corpse raising stays quarantined (don't use yet). Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Ghostwalk — MysticismspellConditionalInvisibility returns after you break it. Master Illusion. The current changelog says invisibility refreshes after roughly three seconds. It does not teleport you. Not tested in co-op yet. Source

Circle of Life — MysticismspellAllowedPlace a circle that restores Health inside it. Adept Restoration. Try it as a healing spot; healing other players inside the circle is (not verified). Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Close Greater Wounds — MysticismspellAllowedBurst healing for yourself and nearby living allies. Adept Restoration. An earlier area-healing option than Grand Healing. Its effect on friends is not tested in co-op yet. Source
Direct fire, frost and shock spellsspellAllowedAttack with fire, frost or shock. Flames, Frostbite and Sparks keep Mysticism's extra effects. Flames damages and burns; Intense Flames can cause fear. Frostbite drains health and stamina, slows, and can freeze. Sparks damages; Disintegrate can trigger. Avoid friendly NPCs. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Grand Healing — MysticismspellAllowedKeep casting to heal yourself and nearby allies. Expert Restoration. Mysticism changes the vanilla burst into continuous healing while you cast. It is not a starter spell; not tested in co-op yet. Source
Heal Other — MysticismspellAllowedSend a burst of healing to your target. Apprentice Restoration in Mysticism, earlier than vanilla's Adept version. Aim at a living ally. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Healing Hands — MysticismspellAllowedHold the cast to keep healing your target. Apprentice Restoration. Aim at a living ally and keep casting. Player healing amounts, experience and perk bonuses are not tested in co-op yet. Source
Night Eye — MysticismspellAllowedSee more clearly in the dark. Apprentice Illusion. Any race can learn this spell. Vanilla already gives night vision to Khajiit and vampires. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Ordinary self-healing spellsspellAllowedRestore your own Health with healing spells. Healing and Fast Healing are continuous casts in Mysticism; Close Wounds gives a burst. These do not resurrect players. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Stendarr's Mercy — MysticismspellAllowedA powerful burst of healing for yourself and nearby allies. Master Restoration. A late healing option for living allies nearby. It does not resurrect players. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Sunbeam — MysticismspellAllowedChannel sunlight to damage undead. Novice Restoration. Keep casting to burn an undead target. A starting anti-undead option; not tested in co-op yet. Source
Vampiric Bolt — MysticismspellAllowedFire a bolt that steals Health from a living enemy. Adept Destruction. A ranged drain spell that heals you as it harms its target. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
Vampiric Touch — MysticismspellAllowedSteal Health from a living enemy within melee range. Adept Destruction. The absorbed Health heals you. It does not require raising a corpse. Not tested in co-op yet. Source
