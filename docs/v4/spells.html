Chapter 03 · Spells and magic

Spells by school

Spells by school, before and after

Our spell mod is Mysticism, not Apocalypse. It arrives with our next collection update.
SchoolBefore: vanillaAfter: Mysticism
AlterationProtection and utilityAdds enemy debuffs.
ConjurationBound weapons, summons, raised corpsesMore summons, including skeletons. One active summon per player. HOLD: raising corpses, Mark and Recall.
DestructionElemental attacksMore progression and drain spells, such as Vampiric Bolt and Blood Lance.
IllusionStealth, morale, mind controlMore choices. Enemy-control spells can't control players.
RestorationHealing, wards, undead defenseMore support. Start with Healing Hands and Heal Other; Grand Healing comes later.

Sources: Mysticism
