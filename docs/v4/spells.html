Spells, before and after

Our spell mod is Mysticism, not Apocalypse.
SchoolBefore: vanillaAfter: Mysticism
AlterationProtection and utilityAdds enemy debuffs. HOLD: Mark and Recall.
ConjurationBound weapons, summons, raised corpsesMore summons, including skeletons. One active summon per player. HOLD: raising corpses.
DestructionElemental attacksMore progression and drain spells, such as Vampiric Bolt and Blood Lance.
IllusionStealth, morale, mind controlMore choices. Enemy-control spells can't control players.
RestorationHealing, wards, undead defenseMore support. Start with Healing Hands, Heal Other and Grand Healing.
