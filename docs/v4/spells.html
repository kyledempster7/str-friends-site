03 · Spells and magic

Spells by school

Page 1 of 2: Spells by school · Helping friends with magic Next: Helping friends with magic →

Listen to this section: Episode 7, The magic path, part one: attack and control · Episode 8, The magic path, part two: healing and summoning · Episode 12, What to go looking for (opens in a new tab)(each opens the Audio guide in a new tab)

Vanilla means the unmodded game. Mysticism changes all five spell schools. Some spells and summons use Better Vanilla Perks instead. Not tested in co-op yet. Collection revision 7.
SchoolVanillaModsOur rule
AlterationFlesh armor, light, detection, Telekinesis and Transmute. Paralyze and Mass Paralysis belong here.Burden slows enemies. Corrode Armor weakens armor. Feather increases carrying capacity. Sea Stride allows water-walking. Paralysis moves to Illusion. Better Vanilla Perks: Candlelight and its scroll last 120 seconds.No named spell quarantine here. Mark and Recall belong to Conjuration.
ConjurationBound weapons, familiars, atronachs, Dremora and corpse raising. Dawnguard adds Soul Cairn undead summons.Adds Skeleton summons, bound weapons and Daedric Cure for summons. Mark and Recall add Expert travel spells. Conjure Familiar keeps Mysticism's spell and wolf. Better Vanilla Perks supplies its text and look.Quarantined (don't use yet): Mark, Recall and corpse raising. Keep one active summon per player.
DestructionFire, frost and shock attacks. Vampires can absorb Health using Vampiric Drain.Burning Touch adds close-range fire. Greater Flames extends continuous attacks. Vampiric Touch, Vampiric Bolt, Blood Lance steal living enemies' Health. Flames, Frostbite and Sparks keep Mysticism's effects. Flames damages and burns; Intense Flames can cause fear. Frostbite drains health and stamina, slows, and can freeze. Sparks damages; Disintegrate can trigger.No named spell quarantine. Exact co-op effects (not verified).
IllusionCalm, fear, frenzy, morale, Muffle and Invisibility. Paralysis is an Alteration effect.Paralyze moves here. Silence stops enemy casting. Command controls NPCs. Night Eye grants dark vision. Ghostwalk refreshes invisibility after it breaks.Enemy-control spells cannot control human players. Exact co-op effects (not verified).
RestorationHealing Hands channels healing onto NPCs. Heal Other gives an Adept burst. Grand Healing is an Expert area burst.Heal Other becomes Apprentice. Close Greater Wounds adds Adept area healing. Grand Healing stays Expert but channels. Sunbeam damages undead. Circle of Life heals. Stendarr's Aura, Poison Rune: partly Better Vanilla Perks effects.Ordinary healing allowed. Player healing amounts, circle effects and Resurgence bonuses (not verified).
