Chapter 03 · Spells and magic

Spells by school

Spells by school, before and after

Our spell mod is Mysticism, not Apocalypse. It is part of our collection (revision 6).
SchoolBefore: vanillaAfter: MysticismOur rule
AlterationFlesh armor, light, detection, Telekinesis and Transmute; Paralyze and Mass Paralysis belong here.Burden slows enemies; Corrode Armor weakens armor; Feather increases carrying capacity; Sea Stride allows water-walking. Paralysis moves to Illusion.No named spell quarantine here. Mark and Recall belong to Conjuration.
ConjurationBound weapons, familiars, atronachs, Dremora and corpse raising; Dawnguard adds Soul Cairn undead summons.Adds Skeleton summons, more bound weapons, Daedric Cure for summons, and Expert travel spells Mark and Recall.Quarantined (don't use yet): Mark, Recall and corpse raising. Keep one active summon per player.
DestructionFire, frost and shock attacks; vampires can absorb Health using Vampiric Drain.Burning Touch adds close-range fire; Greater Flames extends channelled attacks. Vampiric Touch, Vampiric Bolt and Blood Lance steal living enemies' Health.No named spell quarantine; exact co-op effects (not verified).
IllusionCalm, fear, frenzy, morale, Muffle and Invisibility; paralysis is an Alteration effect.Paralyze moves here; Silence stops enemy casting; Command controls NPCs; Night Eye grants dark vision; Ghostwalk refreshes invisibility after it breaks.Enemy-control spells cannot control human players. Exact co-op effects (not verified).
RestorationHealing Hands channels healing onto NPCs; Heal Other gives an Adept burst. Grand Healing is an Expert area burst.Heal Other becomes Apprentice; Close Greater Wounds adds Adept area healing. Grand Healing stays Expert but channels. Sunbeam damages undead; Circle of Life heals.Ordinary healing allowed. Player healing amounts, circle effects and Resurgence bonuses (not verified).
