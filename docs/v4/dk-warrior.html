The full race table and its quarantines are on Races

Two options

Suggestions, not assignments: pick the option that sounds fun. The weapon paths and armor perks below work for either race. None of this has been tried together yet.
OptionWhat the race gives youHow you fightHow it fits the party
Nord warriorTwo-Handed +10 and One-Handed +10 at the start. 25% frost resistance. Damage that rises as your Health falls.Greatsword or battleaxe, or a one-handed weapon with a shield. Heavy armor.Stands in front. The healer keeps you standing. The archer and the wizard stay behind you.
Imperial warriorBlock +10 and Heavy Armor +5 at the start. Discipline for nearby allies. Level-up choices that reshape your character.The same two weapon paths. The same heavy armor.Stands in front, in the same place. Discipline may help friends near you. That is not tested with two players.

Path 1: two-handed

Two-handed perks come from Better Vanilla Perks. A two-handed weapon means no shield, so a healer or a friend covers the hard hits.
PickWhat it does in our gameOur rule or note
BarbarianTwo-handed weapons do 20% more damage at rank 1 and 100% more at rank 5. They also ignore 10% of armor at rank 1 and 40% at rank 5. Better greatswords bleed targets as ranks rise.No extra rule
Deep WoundsGreatswords have a 15%, then 25%, then 35% chance of extra critical damage.No extra rule
Axes and hammersLimbsplitter makes battleaxe power attacks do 20% more damage, rising to 50%. Skullcrusher makes warhammers do 20% more damage to armor and undead, rising to 60%.No extra rule
SweepSideways power attacks hit every enemy in front of you. The second rank makes every attack do it.Players can't hurt players. Friendly non-player characters can still be hit.
FlurryYour main-hand attacks are 20% faster. It needs One-Handed or Two-Handed at 30.No extra rule
BlockingBlock with the weapon. Shield perks, such as Deflect Arrows and rank 1 of Strong Reflexes, need a shield.Take Block perks only if you plan to carry a shield.

Path 2: one-handed and shield

This path trades some damage for safety. Armsman still does most of the work.
PickWhat it does in our gameOur rule or note
ArmsmanOne-handed weapons do 20% more damage at rank 1 and 100% more at rank 5, with 15% to 35% extra critical chance.No extra rule
Weapon perksBladesman adds critical chance and bleeding to swords. Bone Breaker makes maces do more damage to armor and undead. Hack and Slash makes war-axe power attacks do more damage.No extra rule

Strong ReflexesRank 1: you take half damage from power attacks while blocking with a shield. Rank 2: you take half damage from every attack while you block.Our held-perk patch removed rank 2's slow-motion effect. The perk menu says "(changed for co-op)".
Deflect ArrowsYou resist 75% of the arrows that hit your shield.No extra rule
BashesDisarming Bash makes power bashes disarm foes and do double damage. Deadly Bash makes bashes with weapons or shields hit much harder.No extra rule

Gear and play

TopicWhat to knowOur rule or note
WeaponsAny weapon from the base game works. Weapon Collection LotR adds 26 craftable weapons. They include two-handed swords such as Narsil, Anduril and Glamdring, Gimli's two-handed axes, and one-handed swords.Optional gear mod. Players without it will not see your weapon, and you will not see theirs.
Fighting styleMortal Enemies makes enemies commit to their swings, so you can sidestep and strike. Precision gives real weapon hitboxes, so wide swings hit several enemies.Both are Required. Take care around friendly non-player characters.
Standing stoneWarrior, as the Two-handed guardian sheet says. Switch to Lord later.Find the stones southwest of Riverwood.
Hunger and thirstEach one halves your health, magicka and stamina regeneration. Food clears Hunger. A potion clears Thirst.They come about 30 real minutes after you last ate or drank.
Perk pointsSkyshards give 1 perk point for every 3 you absorb.Each player collects their own. Not tested with two players.
Shared experienceWhen anyone earns combat experience, everyone gets that amount. Your last-used combat skill gains it.Draw the weapon you want to train before a fight.
