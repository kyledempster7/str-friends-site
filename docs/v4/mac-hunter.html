The Wood Elf racial power and its quarantine are on Races

Two paths

Suggestions, not assignments: pick the path that sounds fun. None of this has been tried together yet.
PathHow you fightMain skillsHow it fits the party
Bow and bladeShoot first. Draw a sword when enemies reach you. One-handed or two-handed.Archery, Sneak, Light Armor, and One-Handed or Two-HandedScouts and opens the fight. Steps in beside the frontliner when enemies close.
Beast companionsShoot from behind a spectral wolf or bear. One beast at a time.Archery, Sneak, ConjurationScouts and opens the fight. The beast keeps enemies busy while you shoot.

What a Wood Elf gets

PartIn our gameOur rule
Starting skillsArchery +10 and Sneak +10. Alchemy, Light Armor, Lockpicking and Pickpocket +5.No extra rule
Racial powerImperious's Wood Elf power is Beast Tongue. It recruits the nearest carnivorous animal.Revision 7: the Friend Kit gives Hunter's Mark, once a day (not tested in the game yet). Quarantined (don't use yet): Beast Tongue.
Other changesEat corpses for attributes. Harrier marks prey and weakens foes. Sense movement while still. Innate poison and disease resistance is gone.Harrier's effects for friends are not verified.

Path 1: bow and blade

Archery perks come from Better Vanilla Perks and our Quiet Hunter patch. Not tested with two players: whether your bow perks count on an enemy another player's game controls.
PickWhat it does in our gameOur rule or note
Archery: OverdrawBows do more damage with each rank. Ranks 2 to 5 cost 5, 10, 15 and 20% of your melee and unarmed damage.A swordsman pays for this. Stop at rank 1 for full sword damage, or take more and accept weaker swings.
Archery: Quick Draw or Steady HandQuick Draw reloads archery weapons 25% faster. Steady Hand adds 10% bow critical chance and 5% bow damage.Pick one. The Quiet Hunter patch greys out the other.
Archery: Power Shot or RangerPower Shot gives bows 50% armor penetration and a 50% chance to stagger. Ranger moves you faster with a drawn bow and doubles bow bash damage.Pick one. The Quiet Hunter patch greys out the other.
Archery: Critical Shot and BullseyeThey add no critical bonus while you sneak. Sneak attacks keep the Sneak tree's multipliers.Open with a sneak shot. Fight in the open for critical hits.
One-Handed swordArmsman raises damage and adds critical chance. Bladesman adds bleeding. Flurry speeds main-hand attacks.No extra rule
Two-Handed greatswordBarbarian raises damage, adds armor penetration and makes greatswords bleed targets.No extra rule. A greatsword means swapping weapons, so keep the bow ready.
SneakBackstab extends sneak bonuses to melee and spell attacks. Shadow Warrior adds night vision and greatsword sneak damage. Its second rank adds combat invisibility.No extra rule
Light ArmorAgile Defender adds material bonuses, such as cheaper power attacks and stagger resistance. Custom Fit and Matching Set need only three pieces.A Wood Elf starts with +5 Light Armor.
Perk pointsA hybrid spends perks in two trees. Skyshards give 1 perk point for every 3 you absorb.Each player collects their own. Not tested with two players.
Shared experienceWhen anyone earns combat experience, everyone gets that amount. Your last-used combat skill gains it.Draw the bow or the sword before a fight to train the one you want.

Gear for bow and blade

GearWhat it isNote
BowA bow from the base game. Weapon Collection LotR adds no bows.Carry plenty of arrows.
SwordAny sword from the base game. Weapon Collection LotR adds 26 craftable weapons, including swords such as Narsil and Glamdring in one-handed and two-handed versions.Optional gear mod. Players without it will not see your weapon, and you will not see theirs.

Swing carePrecision gives real weapon hitboxes. Wide swings hit several enemies.Take care around friendly non-player characters.

Path 2: beast companions

These come from Mysticism. No other installed mod gives a player a bear or wolf. Spell tomes turn up with vendors and loot. We have not checked in the game where each one appears.
OptionWhat it doesOur rule
Conjure Spectral Wolf (Novice Conjuration)Summons a spectral wolf for 60 seconds. Costs 89 Magicka before perks. Better Vanilla Perks calls the effect Conjure Familiar.Allowed. One active summon. Mysticism puts its spell tome in the Riverwood Trader's chest.
Conjure Spectral Dire Wolf (Apprentice Conjuration)Summons a spectral dire wolf for 60 seconds. Costs 147 Magicka before perks.Allowed. One active summon.
Conjure Spectral Bear (Expert Conjuration)Summons a spectral bear for 60 seconds. Costs 367 Magicka before perks. A new Wood Elf starts with about 100 Magicka.Allowed. One active summon. A late-game spell.
Beast Tongue (Wood Elf power)Befriends the nearest carnivorous animal as a companion.Quarantined (don't use yet). Use the spells above instead.

Beasts in co-op, and with the bow

TopicWhat to knowOur rule or note
One at a timeA wolf and a bear together would be two summons. Call one, let it fade, then call the other.One active summon per player.
Who owns itSkyrim Together gives a summon an owner: the player who cast it. It stays with that player.From reading Skyrim Together's code. Not tested here with two players.
Early to lateWolf first. Dire wolf at Apprentice. Bear once you reach Expert.Cast from cover, then shoot while the beast fights.
Conjuration perksSummoner and Puppeteer let you cast summons twice and three times as far away. Twin Souls makes Conjuration spells cost 10% less Magicka.They add no summon slots in our game.
Archery perksWith no sword, Overdraw ranks 2 to 5 cost you nothing that matters. Quick Draw or Steady Hand, and Power Shot or Ranger, work as on the other path.Pick one of each pair, as on the other path.
Healing the beastDaedric Cure heals nearby summoned helpers.Use it on your one allowed summon.
Magicka and StaminaThe beast spends Magicka. The bow spends Stamina. Split your levelling between both.No extra rule
