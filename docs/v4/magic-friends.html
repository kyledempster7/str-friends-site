Not yet tried together: how much healing reaches other players, and what the buffs do for them.
SpellSingle playerCo-op
Healing Hands, Heal OtherHeal NPCs such as your followerAlso reach other players.
Courage, Rally, Call to ArmsBuff your followersSkyrim Together sends these to other players too.
Circle of LifeGuardian Circle heals only youA healing circle.
Calm, Fear, FrenzyWork on enemies and other NPCsStill work on enemies. They can't control players.
Mark and RecallTeleport back to a marked spotQuarantined: don't use it until we test it together.
Auras, undead-healing spells, stored spells and other automatic castsCast by themselves or on a triggerNot cleared by normal healing support. Start with direct spells and ordinary healing. Remote healing amount and targeting still need a host-and-guest test.
Raising corpsesRaise the dead to fight for youQuarantined: don't use it until we test it together. One active summon per player instead. An ordinary conjured undead is the first test candidate, within that one summon. It is not a promise of flawless sync.
