October 7: Five Races patch

The Friend Kit has a new small plugin, STR-FiveRaces-Stability.esp. It makes Imperious safer for a shared world. Every player needs it.

  • Racial goals are no longer shared. Finishing your High Elf, Breton, Imperial, Nord or Wood Elf goal used to hand the power to everyone in the party. Now it stays in your own game.
  • Contingency (High Elf) and Beast Tongue (Wood Elf) are locked. The goals that earned them are switched off, so you cannot get them. Harrier's armor mark in combat still works.
  • Shared Ancestry (Breton) can no longer borrow Contingency, Beast Tongue, Spirit Walk or Red Sand Dance.
  • NPCs no longer cast Harrier, Avatar, Colovian Star, Spirit Walk or Ancestral Guardian on their own.
  • Do not use the Imperious menu option "Debug: manual". It still lets a player give themselves any power.
  • Not tested in the game yet. A two-player check is on the test list.
  • Friend Kit: the new zip is STR-Friend-Kit-2026-10-07.zip. It is still one zip and one double-click, and it now builds STR-Patches.zip with five private plugins. Extract it and double-click START HERE - double-click me.bat again, then install the new STR-Patches.zip in Vortex. Races has the five rows this changes.
  • The three race plugins are also on Nexus: Race Power Fallback, Hunter's Mark and Five Races Stability. Use the Friend Kit copy, not both.

October 6: revision 7

Revision 7 is our current list of mods and Friend Kit patches. This is what it adds and changes.

  • New required mods: Random Dragons No More, Cathedral Weathers and Seasons, Disable Fast Travel, Simplest Survival (with Keyword Item Distributor and our 30-minute patch), The Notice Board, Vampire Attacks Restored (with our Gentle Start patch), Elysium Estate, Skyrim Trading Posts, Skyrim Bandit Expansion, Skyshards and PapyrusUtil. Our mods lists all 38.
  • New optional mods: Sounds of Skyrim Complete, Playable Greybeard Robes, Wizard Hats - Simple, Weapon Collection LotR and Harborwatch.
  • In the game: map fast travel is off. Hunger and thirst arrive about 30 real minutes after you eat or drink. Vampires attack towns at night, never before level 12. Random dragon attacks are off.
  • Friend Kit: one zip, STR-Friend-Kit-2026-10-06.zip, and one double-click. Extract it and double-click START HERE - double-click me.bat. It builds STR-Patches.zip (four private plugins) and runs the Creation Club cleanup.
  • Perks: our patches changed some Better Vanilla Perks perks. Strong Reflexes rank 2, Steady Hand, Dragonborn ranks 4 and 5 and Resurgence are no longer quarantined. Torch unlocking and the extra summon slots are removed, so keep one active summon. Archery asks you to pick one of each pair: Steady Hand or Quick Draw, Power Shot or Ranger. Dark Souls 2 and Necromage stay locked. Skills says how.
  • Races: the race patch gives High Elves Highborn and Redguards Adrenaline Rush back, once a day. It is tested alone, not yet with two players. New: Wood Elves get Hunter's Mark, 20 seconds of double bow damage that marks what your arrows hit, once a day. It is not tested in the game yet. Dark Elf still has no racial power. Races says more.
  • Setup and joining: First-time setup and Joining a session are now numbered steps. Joining has a new section, Leaving and coming back later.
