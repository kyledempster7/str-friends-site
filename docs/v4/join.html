Chapter 04 · A night of adventure

Joining a session

Quick answer

QuestionShort answer
How do I join tonight?Launch Skyrim Together, load your campaign character, press F2 and choose Connect, check you're in the leader's party, then type READY.

Joining, before and after

StepBefore: single playerAfter: our co-op
StartPress Play on SteamLaunch Skyrim Together (SkyrimTogether.exe).
LoadLoad any saveLoad your campaign character, never a solo save.
ConnectNot neededPress F2 or Right Ctrl and choose Connect. Use the server address and password the host sends you privately. If the host asked you to run a connection app from our Nexus page, start it first.
PartyNot neededThe leader presses F2, clicks Player Manager, then the Party Menu tab, and clicks Launch party. Everyone else joins automatically once connected.
Check your partyNot neededPress F2, click Player Manager, then the Party Menu tab. It lists your party by Name, Lv. and Location. If the leader or you are missing, tell the leader before you start.
Meet upJust playGo to the spot the host names on Discord. Then open chat with F2 or Right Ctrl, type READY and press Enter.
If it failsReload or restartStop and send the host the exact error message. Never post the server details publicly.
