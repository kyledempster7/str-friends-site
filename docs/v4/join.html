Create the party (leader)Not neededLeader: press F2. Open Player Manager, then Party Menu. Click Launch party. Connected players join automatically.
Find everyone in the partyNot neededPress F2. Open Player Manager, then Party Menu. Check Name, Lv. (level) and Location for everyone. Missing someone? Tell the leader.
Accept a missing invitationNot neededIn Player Manager, open Invites. Choose "Accept invite from" your leader. Still missing? Tell the leader.
