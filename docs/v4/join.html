04 · Setup, joining and leaving

Joining a session

Page 3 of 5: First-time setup · Tailscale · Joining a session · While playing · Leaving Next: While playing →

Listen to this section: Episode 2, Joining and leaving (opens in a new tab)(each opens the Audio guide in a new tab)

  1. Check your load order

    1. Close Vortex and Skyrim.
    2. Double-click Check load order.bat (it is in STR-Load-Order.zip).
    3. The last line must say RESULT: MATCH. If it says DIFFERENT, do not connect. Send the host a screenshot.

    Run it before every session. Changing any mod can change the order.

  2. Launch the co-op game

    Double-click your configured SkyrimTogether.exe shortcut.

  3. Load your campaign character

    Load your campaign character, never a solo save.

  4. Get connection details on Discord

    Install Tailscale. Then accept the host's invite, sent privately on Discord.

  5. Connect

    1. Press F2 or Right Ctrl.
    2. Choose Connect.
    3. Enter the server details the host sends privately on Discord.

    Keep passwords and addresses out of public screenshots and posts.

  6. Create the party (leader)

    1. Leader: press F2.
    2. Open Player Manager, then Party Menu.
    3. Click Launch party. Connected players join automatically.

  7. Check the party

    1. Press F2. Open Player Manager, then Party Menu.
    2. Check Name, Lv. (level) and Location for everyone.
    3. Missing your invite? In Player Manager, open Invites and choose "Accept invite from" your leader.
    4. Still missing someone? Tell the leader.

  8. Meet and say READY

    1. Go to the spot the host names on Discord.
    2. Press F2 or Right Ctrl.
    3. Type READY. Press Enter.

  9. Connection problem?

    Stop. Send the host the exact error message. Keep server details private.

Leaving and coming back later

  • The server keeps no save of your character or the world.
  • Your save holds your character and your copy of the world.
  • The server keeps the clock while it runs. A restart resets the clock.
  • Leaving: save, disconnect, then quit to the main menu. Leaving has the steps.
  • Coming back: load your latest save, then connect.
  • The sky racing to the server's hour on join is normal.
  • Your date may differ from your friends' dates. That is normal too.
  • The server is its own program on the host's PC. Friends can play while the host is out of the game. This is likely from the mod's code. Nobody has tried it yet.
  • Playing apart: the first player to load an area decides what is there.
  • A dead NPC can come back alive when you rejoin. A chest outside a home can be replaced by a friend's version.
  • Quest steps only reach players who are connected at that moment. Nobody catches up later.
  • All of this is read from the mod's code. We have not seen it in a game with the full group.

Group rules

  • Play the group character only while connected.
  • Keep a separate character for solo play.
  • Only the leader uses console fixes on NPCs. The host has the steps (The party leader).
  • Stash loot only in player homes.
