Quick answer

QuestionShort answer
How do I join tonight?Double-click your configured SkyrimTogether.exe shortcut. Load your campaign character. Press F2. Choose Connect. Follow the party steps below.

  1. Launch the co-op game

    Double-click your configured SkyrimTogether.exe shortcut.

  2. Load your campaign character

    Load your campaign character, never a solo save.

  3. Get connection details on Discord

    Install Tailscale. Then accept the host's invite, sent privately on Discord.

  4. Connect

    1. Press F2 or Right Ctrl.
    2. Choose Connect.
    3. Enter the server details the host sends privately on Discord.

    Keep passwords and addresses out of public screenshots and posts.

  5. Create the party (leader)

    1. Leader: press F2.
    2. Open Player Manager, then Party Menu.
    3. Click Launch party. Connected players join automatically.

  6. Check the party

    1. Press F2. Open Player Manager, then Party Menu.
    2. Check Name, Lv. (level) and Location for everyone.
    3. Missing your invite? In Player Manager, open Invites and choose "Accept invite from" your leader.
    4. Still missing someone? Tell the leader.

  7. Meet and say READY

    1. Go to the spot the host names on Discord.
    2. Press F2 or Right Ctrl.
    3. Type READY. Press Enter.

  8. Connection problem?

    Stop. Send the host the exact error message. Keep server details private.

Leaving and coming back later

  • Your progress lives in your own save, on your own PC. The server keeps no save of your character or the world.
  • Save before you leave. Then disconnect and quit to the main menu: Leaving has the steps.
  • To come back, load your latest campaign save and connect the usual way. Check the usual leader and regroup before questing. Rejoining with a full group is not tested yet.
  • The server is its own program on the host's PC, so it can run while the host is not in the game. Nobody has tested friends playing without the host in the game yet.
  • Playing your campaign character alone, offline, moves your save on. Quests you advance alone may not match the group's. Our rule: do not advance the main campaign without missing players, and do not quest alone to catch up (The party leader). For solo time, use a separate character, as a smaller-party night does.
