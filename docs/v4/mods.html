Required and Optional

How we chose the marks: we read each mod's files, Skyrim Together's code and other players' compatibility reports. We have not tested a group where some players have a mod and others do not. Read "nobody else is affected" as what the evidence says, not as something we have watched happen.
MarkMeansWhat to do
RequiredIt changes things all players share: items, spells, perks, races, quests, spawns or weather. Or another mod needs it.Install it. Everyone has the same Required mods.
OptionalIt changes only what you see, hear or how your own menus, map and camera work. Gear mods are the exception: see the next row.Skip it if you like. Nobody else is affected, unless it is a gear mod.
Optional gear modsPlayable Greybeard Robes, Wizard Hats - Simple and Weapon Collection LotR add craftable gear.Skipping one changes what you see on other players. They will not see your gear either. Handing gear to a player without the mod has not been tested.
Vanilla means the unmodded game. The collection on Nexus is revision 7: these 38 installed mods, 26 Required and 12 Optional. The older collection had 19 mods. Two private perk patches and two private race patches from the Friend Kit come on top. Mods marked new are not in the older collection.
ModVanillaModsRequired or OptionalWhy
Skyrim Together RebornPlay aloneAdds co-op connections, parties, chat and shared quests.RequiredThe multiplayer client that everything else sits on.
Skyrim Script Extender (SKSE)Standard game scriptingAdds scripting support needed by our mods.RequiredIt runs every mod that ships as a plugin file, including Skyrim Together.
Address Library for SKSE PluginsNo SKSE plugin support libraryHelps SKSE plugins work with the game.RequiredSkyrim Together and most of our plugin mods look up game code through it.
PapyrusUtil SENo extra scripting helpersA helper that QuickLoot needs for its value and weight columns.Required · new since revision 6QuickLoot IE needs it, even though QuickLoot IE itself is Optional.
SkyUIOld inventory menusSortable menus and the Mod Configuration menu.RequiredMCM Helper needs it, so the two fail together.
MCM HelperSome mod menus missingSupports mod settings in the Mod Configuration menu.RequiredThe settings pages for Precision, True Directional Movement, TrueHUD and Better Third Person Selection need it.
TrueHUDOne health bar for your current targetHealth bars over enemies and a boss bar.RequiredTrue Directional Movement needs it for its lock-on marker.
Helgen ExpressThe long Helgen introQuick choices for race, sign, gear, spells and helper.RequiredIt changes the start of the game for every character.
Mortal EnemiesEnemies spin mid-swingEnemies commit to swings, so you can sidestep and punish.RequiredIt changes how every enemy moves. Players with different data would fight different enemies.
PrecisionFuzzy hit detectionReal weapon hitboxes. Wide swings hit several enemies. Take care around friendly NPCs.RequiredIt changes how hits are detected, not only how they look. Nobody has tested a player without it.
MysticismVanilla spellsAdds spells across all five schools. Some spells use Better Vanilla Perks. See Spells by schoolRequiredIt adds hundreds of spells, effects and books that every player must share.
Better Vanilla PerksVanilla perk treesExpanded perk choices while keeping the familiar trees. We chose it for the first campaign. Two Friend Kit patches change some of its perks. See SkillsRequiredIt rewrites perks, spells and weapons for everyone.
ImperiousVanilla racial bonusesNew powers and passives for every race. See RacesRequiredIt changes every race, and the race patch needs it.
Random Dragons No MoreDragons attack at random while you travelSwitches off the random dragon attacks. Quest dragons stay.Required · new since revision 6It only works if the party leader's game has it, and anyone can lead.
Cathedral Weathers and SeasonsVanilla weatherReplaces weather, fog and thunder, and makes nights darker.Required · new since revision 6The party leader's weather is what everyone sees. The Sounds of Skyrim patch needs it.
Disable Fast TravelClick a map marker to travel there instantly.Switches off map fast travel. Carriages and ferries should still work, but we have not tested them.Required · new since revision 6Everyone travels the same roads together.
Keyword Item DistributorNo helper for tagging items.Tags food and potions so Simplest Survival can tell them apart. Nothing to see in play.Required · new since revision 6Simplest Survival does nothing without it.
Simplest SurvivalNo hunger or thirst.Adds hunger and thirst. Each halves your health, magicka and stamina regeneration until you eat food or drink a potion.Required · new since revision 6Everyone needs the same survival rules.
Simplest Survival - Needs 30 Minutes PatchNo hunger or thirst.Hunger and thirst arrive about 30 real minutes after you last ate or drank. That is 10 game hours; a game day is about 72 real minutes.Required · new since revision 6It sets the same timing for everyone. It is a separate mod page on Nexus.
The Notice BoardJobs come from talking to people.Adds job boards outside inns. The leader takes a job and the group goes together. The other players may not see the job in their journals.Required · new since revision 6It adds quests and boards to the shared world. Who gets the reward is not tested.
Vampire Attacks Restored Outside of Walled CitiesVampire attacks on towns are switched off.Brings back vampire attacks on towns and villages at night, outside the walled cities.Required · new since revision 6It changes what spawns in the shared world. How an attack looks for the other players is not tested.
Vampire Attacks Restored - Gentle Start PatchVampire attacks on towns are switched off.Our patch for the mod above. No attack before level 12. At least three game days between attacks. Less likely until level 30.Required · new since revision 6Everyone needs the same attack rules. It is a separate mod page on Nexus and needs the mod above.
Elysium Estate SSENo group house.A player house southwest of Whiterun, with crafting rooms, a kitchen, stables and display cases. It is our group house.Required · new since revision 6It adds a building to the shared world, so everyone needs it. Its inside areas count as player homes, so each player's chests there are private.
Skyrim Trading Posts RemasteredNo trading posts on the roads.Adds seven trading posts along the roads of Skyrim. Merchants buy and sell all kinds of items, and hired guards protect them. A reason to travel on foot now that map fast travel is off. Pathing is untested near the Eastmarch post, by Caravan's End Tower: under our load order, guards or players may walk through some of its buildings.Required · new since revision 6It adds buildings, merchants and guards to the shared world, so everyone needs it.
Skyrim Bandit ExpansionOnly the bandit camps the game ships with.Adds 14 new bandit camps and 2 occupied mines across Skyrim. One mine is a frozen lair. The other is a gold and iron mine held by an Orcish gang.Required · new since revision 6It adds enemies and places to the shared world, so everyone needs it.
SkyshardsPerk points come from levelling up.Hides 108 Skyshards across the nine holds. Every 3 you absorb give 1 perk point. Each player collects their own.Required · new since revision 6It changes how perk points are earned, so everyone needs the same rules. Not tested in co-op.
Skyrim Souls REMenus pause the gameMenus stay open during play. Enemies can still hit you.OptionalMenus only. Skip it and your inventory, map and magic menus behave as they do without the mod. Nobody else is affected.
SSE Display Tweaks60 FPS, cursor escapesBorderless window, higher frame rates, cursor stays in the game.OptionalDisplay only. Skip it and you do not get the borderless window or the frame and physics settings. Nobody else is affected.
Better Third Person SelectionAim the tiny crosshairWalk up. Press E on what's highlighted.OptionalInteraction only. Skip it and in third person you must aim straight at an object, door or container to use it.
True Directional MovementManual turningMove freely. Press middle mouse to lock onto hostiles. Scroll to switch targets.OptionalCamera and movement only. Skip it and you move and lock on as normal Skyrim does, with no lock-on marker. If you install it, keep its hostile-only targeting setting on. TrueHUD stays installed either way.
QuickLoot IEOpen every containerChests and bodies show loot. Press E for one item. Press R for all.OptionalLooting only. Skip it and you open containers and bodies with the normal menu.
moreHUDGuess what you knowShows known enchantments, read books, value and weight.OptionalInformation only. Skip it and you do not see extra details when you look at items.
Atlas Map MarkersSparse mapHundreds of extra map markers.OptionalMap only. Skip it and your map shows only the vanilla markers.
Sounds of Skyrim CompleteVanilla ambient soundAdds ambient sound in the wild, in caves, in ruins and at night. It comes with a patch for Cathedral Weathers.Optional · new since revision 6Sound only. Skip it and you do not hear the added ambient sounds. The patch comes with this mod, so skipping the mod skips the patch too. Cathedral Weathers stays installed either way, because it is Required.
Playable Greybeard RobesNo craftable Greybeard clothingCraftable Greybeard clothing.Optional · new since revision 6Skip it and you will not see this clothing on players who have it, and they will not see yours. Handing it to a player without the mod has not been tested.
Wizard Hats - SimpleNo craftable wizard hats22 craftable hats.Optional · new since revision 6Skip it and you will not see the hats on players who have them, and they will not see yours. Handing a hat to a player without the mod has not been tested.
Weapon Collection LotRNo craftable Lord of the Rings weapons26 craftable weapons.Optional · new since revision 6Skip it and you will not see these weapons on players who have them, and they will not see yours. Handing a weapon to a player without the mod has not been tested.
HarborwatchNo group house in Solitude.A medium house in Solitude, between the Winking Skeever and Angeline's Aromatics. Its author built it as a base for Skyrim Together. Chests inside are private to each player (read from its files and our server settings; not tested in the game).Optional · new since revision 6It is a house, not a rule change. What a player without it sees at that spot is not tested.
