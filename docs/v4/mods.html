Required and Optional

How we chose the marks: we read each mod's files, Skyrim Together's code and other players' compatibility reports. We have not tested a group where some players have a mod and others do not. Read "nobody else is affected" as what the evidence says, not as something we have watched happen.
MarkMeansWhat to do
RequiredIt changes things all players share: items, spells, perks, races, quests, spawns or weather. Or another mod needs it.Install it. Everyone has the same Required mods.
OptionalIt changes only what you see, hear or how your own menus, map and camera work.Skip it if you like. Nobody else is affected.
Optional gear modsPlayable Greybeard Robes, Wizard Hats - Simple and Weapon Collection LotR add craftable gear.Skipping one changes what you see on other players. They will not see your gear either. Handing gear to a player without the mod has not been tested.
Vanilla means the unmodded game. The collection on Nexus is still revision 6, with 19 mods. The next collection revision is these 27 installed mods, all on the host's PC. The list is still growing. Mods marked new are not in revision 6 and are not on Nexus yet.
