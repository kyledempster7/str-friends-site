Group dynamics

Make room for everyone

Protect casting space. Finish threats. Give everyone a turn.

One leader

Fixed for the whole campaign.

Your turn

Choose together; the leader handles quests.

Chat

Typing gets time and acknowledgement.

Live world

Menus and chat do not pause danger.

One leader per playthrough

Keep one leader for the entire campaign: quest NPCs, scenes, items and entering locations first. Everyone can fight bosses and solve puzzles.

Pass the story spotlight

Share story choices without rotating leadership. A different leader needs a separate campaign and characters.

On me

A pursuer? Move toward the group; the frontliner intercepts. No permanent aggro promise.

Healing here

Come within reach. Healing is not resurrection; wards do not guarantee group protection.

Caster first

Focus the named threat. Keep a retreat route open.

Typing counts

F2 or Right Ctrl; click chat, type, then Enter. Ordinary messages are global. Try /party READY or /local Hello. Four-player delivery and readability are not tested yet.

READY or HOLD

READY: ready to proceed. HOLD: find safety and wait.

HELP or BRB

HELP: help now; give location. BRB: taking a break.

Wait for typing

Watch and acknowledge chat. Automatic text-to-speech is not promised.

Our shared setup

Use matching mods. Menus stay live. Target lock selects hostiles; aim heals manually. Player friendly fire is off; neutral characters remain vulnerable. Review additions together.
