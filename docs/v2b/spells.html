Spells and magic

Find your kind of magic

Mysticism adds spells across all five schools. Some spells and summon stats come from Better Vanilla Perks.

Five schools; no new skill tree.

One active summon per player.

Start with ordinary healing spells.

Mark and Recall are quarantined (don't use yet).

Quarantined — don't use yet

Start with familiar spells

Mysticism rebalances spells and adds choices. Buy tomes from usual vendors. Read each tooltip and try direct spells first. Some spells use Better Vanilla Perks instead.

Conjure; don't raise bodies

An ordinary conjured skeleton differs from a reanimated corpse. Our one-summon limit is a test budget, not an engine limit. Extra perk capacity does not override it. Conjure Familiar uses Better Vanilla Perks' magic effect. Potent flame, frost and storm atronachs and their thralls use its stats.

Heal a friend

Start with Healing Hands, Heal Other or Grand Healing. Ordinary healing and Courage, Rally and Call to Arms have player-target support; exact targeting, experience and perk scaling are not tested yet.

Know your support limits

Custom auras and undead-healing spells are not automatically player heals. Imperial Discipline is not a heal. Staff builds are welcome; extra staff skill experience is not included.

Five magic schools

School
Vanilla
Our installed spells
Alteration
Vanilla

Flesh armor, light, detection, Telekinesis and Transmute; Paralyze and Mass Paralysis belong here.

Our installed spells

Burden slows enemies; Corrode Armor weakens armor; Feather increases carrying capacity; Sea Stride allows water-walking. Paralysis moves to Illusion. No named spell quarantine here. Mark and Recall belong to Conjuration. Candlelight and its scroll last 120 seconds with Better Vanilla Perks.

Conjuration
Vanilla

Bound weapons, familiars, atronachs, Dremora and corpse raising; Dawnguard adds Soul Cairn undead summons.

Our installed spells

Adds Skeleton summons, more bound weapons, Daedric Cure for summons, and Expert travel spells Mark and Recall. Quarantined (don't use yet): Mark, Recall and corpse raising. Keep one active summon per player. Conjure Familiar uses Better Vanilla Perks' magic effect. Potent flame, frost and storm atronachs and their thralls use its stats.

Destruction
Vanilla

Fire, frost and shock attacks; vampires can absorb Health using Vampiric Drain.

Our installed spells

Burning Touch adds close-range fire; Greater Flames extends channelled attacks. Vampiric Touch, Vampiric Bolt and Blood Lance steal living enemies' Health. No named spell quarantine; exact co-op effects (not verified). Flames and Frostbite keep one effect each with Better Vanilla Perks, in either hand. Mysticism's extra effects are absent. Sparks uses Better Vanilla Perks casting settings; its effect values match Mysticism.

Illusion
Vanilla

Calm, fear, frenzy, morale, Muffle and Invisibility; paralysis is an Alteration effect.

Our installed spells

Paralyze moves here; Silence stops enemy casting; Command controls NPCs; Night Eye grants dark vision; Ghostwalk refreshes invisibility after it breaks. Enemy-control spells cannot control human players. Exact co-op effects (not verified).

Restoration
Vanilla

Healing Hands channels healing onto NPCs; Heal Other gives an Adept burst. Grand Healing is an Expert area burst.

Our installed spells

Heal Other becomes Apprentice; Close Greater Wounds adds Adept area healing. Grand Healing stays Expert but channels. Sunbeam damages undead; Circle of Life heals. Ordinary healing allowed. Player healing amounts, circle effects and Resurgence bonuses (not verified). Stendarr's Aura, its light and Poison Rune use Better Vanilla Perks effects.

Main Mysticism only

Quarantined (don't use yet): Mark, Recall and corpse reanimation.
