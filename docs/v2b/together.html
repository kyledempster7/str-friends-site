A shared world

What changes in multiplayer?

Your character, your save, one shared campaign.

Everyone saves their own character.

One leader for the entire campaign.

Danger keeps moving.

Respawn; don't reload.

Your save stays yours

Save often. The server does not keep a replacement save for you.

Follow the campaign leader

The same leader handles quest dialogue, scenes and items, and enters locations first. Everyone can fight and solve puzzles together.

Get safe before opening menus

Our setup does not pause. Step away from danger before choosing gear or a potion.

Death is a setback

Normally respawn nearby outdoors or at the indoor entrance. Extra gold-loss penalty: zero. Death may also pay your crime bounties. This exact party setup is not tested yet.

Master difficulty, shared combat experience

Master is enforced. Enemies do not automatically scale with party size. Combat experience sharing is on; crafting is separate.

Protect friendly NPCs

Player-versus-player damage and killcams are off. Lock-on targets hostiles only. Followers and townsfolk can still be hurt.

Keep this campaign together

Keep the party's characters together in level and quest progress. Use separate characters for other campaigns, including smaller-party nights. Finish Helgen offline before connecting.
