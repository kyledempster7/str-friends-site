Unusual builds

Make room for specialists

Choose a useful specialty; friends cover your gaps. These are flexible build ideas.

Choose

Friends cover your gaps.

Give and ask

Every role helps and needs help.

Check first

Ideas are not tested combinations.

Healer or paladin

Restoration first; add personal defense. A paladin swaps between shield, weapon and healing. Give: recovery. Ask: protect my casts. Ordinary robes or steel work; legendary gear can wait.

Two-handed guardian

Greatsword, Heavy Armor and Block make space. Turn to protect a chased friend. Give: breathing room. Ask: answer distant casters. No guaranteed taunt; weapon blocking lacks shield-only defenses.

Stealth ranger

Archery and Sneak find the opening. Keep moving and shooting after discovery. Give: scouting and focused damage. Ask: a quiet opening, then cover. Start with leather and a bow; sneak bonuses are not guaranteed.

Gandalf-style wizard

Destruction and Alteration supply fire and protection; add backup Restoration. Earn a staff later. Give: ranged magic and guidance. Ask: casting space; warn me before scouting. Robes suffice. Extra costume mods need collection approval.

Gravewarden

Restoration first, Conjuration second. One conjured helper buys healing time; raising corpses is different. Give: recovery and distraction. Ask: room for the helper. Corpse raising stays held, even in a darker story.

Storyteller or controller

Speech, Restoration and a weapon suit a skald; enemy control needs a reliable damage fallback. Give: support and shared decisions. Ask: respect control calls. No promised instruments or buff songs. The quest face remains the fixed leader.

Keep the role fun

Keep a fallback. Revisit your specialty after two sessions; discuss major changes so someone still covers healing.

Not tested yet

Healing experience, ally perk bonuses, added summons and full combinations are not tested yet. Check installed tooltips and the rules before spending points.
