What belongs to you

Gear, gold and homes

Your save, pack and gold are personal.

Personal

Your save, your gear, your gold.

Home chest

Test your exact chest before storing valuables.

Hand-offs

Transfers are not tested yet.

Gear and loot

Offer upgrades; leader takes quest items. Stop on missing or duplicate items.

Gold

No party purse. Zero percentage death penalty; crime fines still apply.

Check home storage

Recognized vanilla/DLC home chests should stay local. Test yours with a cheap item, save and rejoin. Privacy and permanence aren't guaranteed. Your friend's chest isn't a delivery box.

Meeting place, not bank

Shared containers aren't permanent banks. Check guild, outdoor and modded storage separately. Empty rooms before renovations replace containers.

Try a small hand-off

  1. 1

    Choose an unowned container outside a home; start with one cheap item.

  2. 2

    Donor uses the full menu, deposits, then closes it.

  3. 3

    Recipient opens the full menu and takes the agreed item.

  4. 4

    Both check packs. Stop if counts disagree.

  5. 5

    Save and check again after reconnecting.

Trading is not tested yet

One person at a time. Never trade floor drops. Gold, crafted items and QuickLoot transfers are not tested yet.

Buy a city home

Leader completes prerequisites; buy from the steward. Budget furnishings separately. Never repeat quests alone.

Build with Hearthfire

Hearthfire land: 5,000 gold before building. Draft layouts, then use the carpenter's bench. Shared construction is not tested yet.

Check each character

Nonleader payments, keys, access and furnishings are not tested yet. Promise neither one shared purchase nor four required payments.

Proposed: personal belongings

Proposed, not accepted: our agreement, not the game's rules. Personal gear and gold; no spawning, duplication or reload-farming. Offer upgrades, alternate ties; no leader bonus. Leader handles quest NPCs and items.

Proposed: shared responsibilities

Voluntary shared costs; pledges aren't payments. Name supply holders. Test personal storage. Earn homes; agree buyer and guests. Protect absent players' belongings and story.

Make the company yours

Optional crafting roles or home goals. Mark supplies pledged, held or spent.

Not tested yet

Family systems, horse ownership after reconnecting and custom-item supply chains.
