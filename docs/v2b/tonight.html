A day in the life

Join, play, and leave well

Meet up. Adventure together. Leave when you need.

F2

Connect, then check the party.

One leader

Keep the same leader all campaign.

READY

Meet where the host says, then type READY.

Leaving?

Save, disconnect, quit.

Before your first join

Match the group's collection. Finish Helgen or Helgen Express offline; save outside. Separate campaigns need separate characters.

Join tonight's game

  1. 1

    Launch Skyrim Together; load your latest campaign save.

  2. 2

    Press F2 or Right Ctrl. Choose Connect; use the details the host sent you privately.

  3. 3

    Check your party: press F2, click Player Manager, then the Party Menu tab. It lists your party by Name, Lv. and Location. Make sure the leader is there.

  4. 4

    Not listed? The leader clicks Launch party in the Party Menu, and you join automatically. Still missing? Look under Invites for "Accept invite from" the leader, or tell the leader.

  5. 5

    Go to the spot the host names on Discord. Then open chat with F2 or Right Ctrl, type READY and press Enter.

Arriving late

Join the party and regroup. Do not catch up by questing alone.

Need a minute?

Type HOLD or BRB. Find safety; menus and chat do not pause danger.

Something looks wrong

Stop and tell the host. Never reload while connected or try to fix quests alone. Save before big scenes.

Leaving

Leaving, now or later

Save. Disconnect. Quit.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. If you have a moment, step somewhere safe first. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

Unsaved progress may be lost. Tell the group later; no recap needed.
