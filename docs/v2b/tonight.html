A day in the life

Join, play, and leave well

Meet up. Adventure together. Leave when you need.

F2

Connect, then check the party.

One leader

Keep the same leader all campaign.

READY

Meet up before starting.

Leaving?

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Before your first join

Match the group's collection. Finish Helgen or Helgen Express offline; save outside. Separate campaigns need separate characters.

Join tonight's game

  1. 1

    Launch Skyrim Together; load your latest campaign save.

  2. 2

    Press F2 or Right Ctrl. Choose Connect; use the host's private details.

  3. 3

    Check Player Manager: join the party with the usual leader.

  4. 4

    No party? The fixed leader creates it. Join before progressing quests.

  5. 5

    Meet up, ready a heal, and say or type READY.

Arriving late

Join the party and regroup. Do not catch up by questing alone.

First room

Leader enters first. Ranger scouts; guardian intercepts; wizard attacks; healer keeps retreat space.

Need a minute?

Type HOLD or BRB. Find safety; menus and chat do not pause danger.

If something disagrees

Save before major scenes. If progress disagrees, stop and tell the host. Never reload while connected or repair quests alone.

A normal goodbye

Find safety. Save and let it finish. F2, Disconnect, Proceed; quit. Each person saves locally; the server cannot replace your save.
