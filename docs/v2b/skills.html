Skills and races

Build your character

Eighteen skills, no fixed classes. Better Vanilla Perks changes trees; Imperious replaces racial packages.

Every race can learn every skill.

Use only the agreed collection.

Read changed tooltips before spending.

Eighteen skills

Skill
Vanilla
Better Vanilla Perks
Alchemy
Vanilla

Alchemist strengthens crafted potions and poisons; Snakeblood grants poison resistance.

Better Vanilla Perks

Alchemist adds poison damage to weapon attacks; Snakeblood adds waterbreathing; Poisoner makes melee poison doses last longer.

Alteration
Vanilla

Novice through Master perks reduce spell costs; Magic Resistance provides separate magic defense.

Better Vanilla Perks

Novice Alteration adds poison resistance; Apprentice adds frost resistance, Adept fire, Expert shock, and Master disease resistance.

Archery
Vanilla

Overdraw raises bow damage; Power Shot adds stagger; Steady Hand slows time while zooming.

Better Vanilla Perks

Overdraw adds armor penetration and longer arrow poisons; Power Shot adds penetration; Bullseye adds critical damage and disarming. Quarantined (don't use yet): the whole Steady Hand branch, starting at Archery 40.

Block
Vanilla

Shield Wall improves blocking; Quick Reflexes slows time while blocking a power attack.

Better Vanilla Perks

Shield Wall becomes Iron Will, adding unarmed damage. Strong Reflexes rank 1 halves power-attack damage when shield-blocking; rank 2 adds slowdown. Quarantined (don't use yet): Strong Reflexes rank 2, Block 50. Rank 1, Block 30, is allowed.

Conjuration
Vanilla

Summoner increases casting distance. Twin Souls allows two summons. Elemental Potency strengthens atronachs. An atronach is a summoned elemental spirit.

Better Vanilla Perks

Summoner and Puppeteer add summon slots; Twin Souls adds two. Staff of the Atronach strengthens certain spells. Potent atronachs and thralls scale with your level. One active summon per player. Quarantined (don't use yet): corpse raising and Dark Souls 2 explosions.

Destruction
Vanilla

Augmented Flames, Frost and Shock add equal damage bonuses; Rune Master increases placement range.

Better Vanilla Perks

Augmented Flames/Frost/Shock rank 1 adds 20/30/40% damage and reduces spell costs; Rune Master also doubles rune capacity.

Enchanting
Vanilla

Enchanter strengthens new enchantments; Soul Siphon recharges weapons from creature kills.

Better Vanilla Perks

Enchanter also recharges weapons from nearby kills; Corpus Enchanter strengthens additional enchantments; Soul Siphon stacks with Enchanter.

Heavy Armor
Vanilla

Juggernaut raises armor rating; Well Fitted needs heavy armor on head, chest, hands and feet.

Better Vanilla Perks

Juggernaut adds material-specific arrow, stagger or magic protection. Well Fitted and Matching Set need three pieces, freeing your helmet choice.

Illusion
Vanilla

Novice Illusion reduces spell cost; Quiet Casting requires the Animage and Kindred Mage path.

Better Vanilla Perks

Novice Illusion adds arrow avoidance. Quiet Casting needs 50 Illusion or Sneak, with no earlier perks, and works while sneaking. Enemy-control spells do not control players.

Light Armor
Vanilla

Agile Defender raises armor rating; Custom Fit requires light armor on head, chest, hands and feet.

Better Vanilla Perks

Agile Defender adds material-specific benefits, including cheaper power attacks or stagger resistance. Custom Fit and Matching Set need only three pieces.

Lockpicking
Vanilla

Novice through Master Locks make their lock difficulties easier to pick.

Better Vanilla Perks

Novice through Master Locks also bypass matching locks with a torch outside combat. Quick Hands no longer needs earlier perks. Quarantined (don't use yet): torch auto-unlock. Use lockpicks manually.

One-Handed
Vanilla

Armsman raises weapon damage; Bladesman adds sword critical hits; Hack and Slash makes axes bleed targets.

Better Vanilla Perks

Armsman also adds critical chance; Bladesman adds bleeding; Hack and Slash boosts axe power attacks. Flurry speeds main-hand attacks.

Pickpocket
Vanilla

Light Fingers improves theft chances; Extra Pockets adds 100 carrying capacity.

Better Vanilla Perks

Light Fingers also improves arrow recovery; Extra Pockets gains additional ranks, reaching 300 extra carrying capacity. Coordinate theft and shared loot.

Restoration
Vanilla

Regeneration strengthens healing by 50%; Recovery improves Magicka regeneration.

Better Vanilla Perks

Regeneration becomes Resurgence, strengthening Restoration by 30%. Mastery perks add Magicka regeneration; Recovery also improves Stamina regeneration. Quarantined (don't use yet): Resurgence until the group's first healer test.

Smithing
Vanilla

Steel Smithing improves steel equipment; Arcane Blacksmith enables tempering enchanted equipment.

Better Vanilla Perks

Basic Smithing also improves iron; Thalmor Smithing adds Falmer crafting; Arcane Blacksmith improves enchanted gear, leather, hide and faction armor.

Sneak
Vanilla

Backstab boosts one-handed sneak attacks; Shadow Warrior briefly breaks detection when crouching in combat.

Better Vanilla Perks

Backstab extends bonuses to melee and spell attacks. Shadow Warrior adds night vision and greatsword sneak damage; its second rank adds combat invisibility.

Speech
Vanilla

Haggling improves prices; Intimidation improves threatening dialogue.

Better Vanilla Perks

Haggling becomes Dragonborn: better prices plus skill-book, summon, steam, drum and merchant benefits. Intimidation also grants Battlecry. Quarantined (don't use yet): Dragonborn rank 4's drum and rank 5's merchant. Rank 2 does not increase our one-summon limit.

Two-Handed
Vanilla

Barbarian raises damage; Limbsplitter makes battleaxes bleed targets; Skullcrusher ignores armor.

Better Vanilla Perks

Barbarian adds armor penetration and greatsword bleeding; Limbsplitter boosts axe power attacks; Skullcrusher adds damage against undead.

Ten races

Race
Vanilla
Imperious
High Elf
Vanilla

+10 Illusion; +5 Alteration, Conjuration, Destruction, Enchanting, Restoration; +50 Magicka. Highborn rapidly restores Magicka for one minute.

Imperious

Level-100 skills improve; low Magicka/Stamina recover faster but weaken defenses; gear weakens, enchantments strengthen. Contingency stores spells for automatic casting. No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Contingency. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).

Argonian
Vanilla

+10 Lockpicking; +5 Alteration, Light Armor, Pickpocket, Restoration, Sneak; disease resistance, waterbreathing, stronger punches. Histskin rapidly restores Health.

Imperious

Faster swimming, waterbreathing, post-swim stealth/speed, low-resource recovery. Caustic Spit strips armor and magic resistance. Innate disease resistance is gone. No quarantined power; co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).

Wood Elf
Vanilla

+10 Archery; +5 Alchemy, Light Armor, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, Sneak; poison/disease resistance. Command Animal temporarily recruits an animal.

Imperious

Eat corpses for attributes; Harrier marks prey and weakens foes; sense movement while still. Beast Tongue recruits animals. Loses innate poison/disease resistance. No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Beast Tongue. Harrier's effects for friends and racial unlocks (not verified).

Breton
Vanilla

+10 Conjuration; +5 Alchemy, Alteration, Illusion, Restoration, Speech; 25% magic resistance, Conjure Familiar. Dragonskin temporarily absorbs hostile spells.

Imperious

15% magic resistance, low-Magicka absorption; special standing-stone bonuses; Grail boosts recovery. Shared Ancestry borrows human or elven racial powers. Borrowed powers keep their quarantines. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).

Dark Elf
Vanilla

+10 Destruction; +5 Alchemy, Alteration, Illusion, Light Armor, Sneak; 50% fire resistance, Sparks. Ancestor's Wrath burns nearby enemies.

Imperious

25% fire resistance; spending Spirit Favor grants armor and further resistance. Whispers marks victims for rewards. Spirit Walk spends Favor to summon ancestors. No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Spirit Walk. Whispers rewards and racial unlocks in co-op (not verified).

Imperial
Vanilla

+10 Restoration; +5 Block, Destruction, Enchanting, Heavy Armor, One-Handed; Imperial Luck finds gold. Voice of the Emperor calms nearby people.

Imperious

Discipline boosts nearby allies' armor/damage; random resource regeneration in combat; permanent attribute/resistance/skill tradeoffs. Colovian Star refills and temporarily boosts Magicka and Stamina. Discipline's benefit to players (not verified); do not count on it as a party buff.

Khajiit
Vanilla

+10 Sneak; +5 Alchemy, Archery, Lockpicking, Pickpocket, One-Handed; stronger claws. Night Eye grants repeatable night vision.

Imperious

Faster movement; sprinting costs extra Stamina. Quieter sneaking, Prowl leaps, stronger claw power attacks. Perception grants night vision and dampens sounds. No quarantined power; co-op movement and racial unlocks (not verified).

Nord
Vanilla

+10 Two-Handed; +5 Block, Light Armor, One-Handed, Smithing, Speech; 50% frost resistance. Battle Cry makes nearby enemies flee.

Imperious

25% frost resistance; choose racial enemies for damage bonuses; missing Health strengthens attacks/spells. Avatar restores Magicka/Stamina and adds frost resistance. No quarantined power; co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).

Orc
Vanilla

+10 Heavy Armor; +5 Block, Enchanting, One-Handed, Smithing, Two-Handed; stronghold access. Berserker Rage doubles physical damage dealt and halves damage received.

Imperious

Bloodlust briefly boosts speed/recovery; Shockwave staggers; gear strengthens but enchantments weaken. Berserk boosts attacks and reduces spell costs; may trigger randomly. No quarantined power; co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).

Redguard
Vanilla

+10 One-Handed; +5 Alteration, Archery, Block, Destruction, Smithing; 50% poison resistance. Adrenaline Rush rapidly restores Stamina.

Imperious

Disease resistance replaces poison resistance; unlimited travel Stamina, recovery when injured, kill bonuses. Red Sand Dance slows time while sprinting. No racial power in our game. Quarantined (don't use yet): Red Sand Dance. Co-op racial effects and unlocks (not verified).

Replacement, not extra bonuses

Imperious bonuses replace vanilla. Powers need racial objectives; NPCs receive abilities. Untested means not tested yet.

Check exact ranks in search

Quarantined (don't use yet): Dark Souls 2; Dragonborn rank 4 drum/rank 5 merchant powers. Strong Reflexes: Block 30 rank 1 allowed; Block 50 rank 2 quarantined (don't use yet).
