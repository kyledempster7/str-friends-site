Single player, before and after

TopicBefore: single playerAfter: our co-op
SavesOne save, reload any timeEveryone keeps their own save. The server holds no copy. Save often; never reload while connected.
QuestsYou talk to everyoneOne party leader for the whole playthrough handles quest people, scenes and quest items, and enters places first. Everyone fights bosses and solves puzzles.
StartingHelgen introFinish Helgen or Helgen Express offline, save outside, then connect.
MenusThe game pausesNothing pauses. Get out of danger before opening menus.
DyingReload a saveYou respawn nearby, or at the entrance indoors. No gold lost. Crime bounties still apply.
DifficultyYour choiceMaster for everyone, set by the server. Enemies do not scale with party size.
Hitting friendsNot a thingPlayers can't hurt players. Followers and townsfolk can still be hurt.
Lock-onAny targetHostiles only.
KillcamsOnOff.
ExperienceAll yoursCombat experience is shared. Crafting experience is not.
Other nightsOne saveA smaller-party night uses separate characters.
TalkingNobody to talk toVoice, or text chat: F2 or Right Ctrl, type, Enter. /party and /local work.

A night of play, before and after

MomentBefore: single playerAfter: our co-op
StartingLoad and playLaunch Skyrim Together, load your campaign save, press F2, Connect with the host's private details, join the leader's party, type READY.
Arriving lateNot a thingJoin the party and regroup. Don't quest alone to catch up.
Need a minutePauseType HOLD or BRB and get somewhere safe. Nothing pauses.
Normal goodbyeSave and quitGet safe. Save and let it finish. F2, Disconnect, Proceed, then quit.
Leave NOWQuitSave if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.
Something looks wrongReloadStop and tell the host. Never reload while connected or fix quests alone.

Ownership, before and after

ThingBefore: single playerAfter: our co-op
Gear and lootAll yoursYours. Offer upgrades before selling. The leader takes quest items.
GoldAll yoursYours. No shared purse. No gold lost on death; crime fines still apply.
Home chestsSafe storageHome chests stay on your own save. Test your exact chest with a cheap item, save and rejoin first.
Shared containersAny chestMeeting places, not banks. Empty rooms before renovating.
Handing items overNot a thingNot tested yet. Try one cheap item in an unowned chest outside a home, then both save and rejoin. Never trade by dropping items.
Buying a city homeDo the quest, pay the stewardThe leader does the quests; buy from the steward. Payments by non-leaders are not tested yet.
Hearthfire landBuy and buildPlots cost 5,000 gold before building. Building together is not tested yet.
