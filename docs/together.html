A familiar world, shared

What changes when we play together?

You still build your own Skyrim character. The adventure follows one party leader, and everyone keeps their own save.

Your character, your save

In solo Skyrim, your save holds your adventure. Here, each player still saves their own character. The server does not keep a replacement save for you. Save often.

One leader for the campaign

In solo play, you make every quest choice. Here, the same party leader handles quest dialogue, quest scenes and quest items, and enters locations first. Everyone can fight and solve puzzles together.

Menus are not a safe place

Solo menus usually pause the world. Our Skyrim Souls setup keeps it moving. Step out of danger before sorting gear or choosing a potion.

Death is a setback

Instead of loading your last save, Skyrim Together normally respawns you nearby outdoors, or at the entrance indoors. Our extra gold-loss penalty is zero; crime bounties are still paid. This exact party setup is not tested yet.

Master difficulty for everyone

The server enforces Master difficulty. Enemies do not automatically scale with party size. Combat experience sharing is on; crafting experience is separate.

Co-op combat

Player-versus-player damage is off. Killcams are off. Target lock is set to hostile targets only. Followers and townsfolk can still be hurt, so watch your swings.

Keep this adventure together

Use separate characters for a different campaign, including a two-player night. Keep the main party's characters together in level and quest progress. Finish the Helgen start offline before connecting.

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

If you cannot save, your latest progress may be lost. Tell the group when you can. You do not need to stay for a recap.

F2 is the F two key. This exit routine is not a proven crash fix. A full rehearsal with this party is not tested yet.
