A spell for your role

Our magic mod is Mysticism

You may have heard Apocalypse mentioned earlier. The selected spell mod is Mysticism. It rebalances familiar spells and adds new choices across the five magic schools; it does not add a new skill tree.

Alteration

Vanilla: protection and utility. Mysticism: more ways to weaken enemies and solve problems. Mark and Recall travel are held.

Conjuration

Vanilla: bound weapons, summoned creatures and raised corpses. Mysticism: more summoned allies, including skeletons. Start with one active summon per player. Raising a dead body is held.

Destruction

Vanilla: elemental attacks. Mysticism: expanded progression and drain spells, such as Vampiric Bolt and Blood Lance. Direct attacks are a starting point for a battle mage.

Illusion

Vanilla: stealth, morale and mind-affecting magic. Mysticism expands those choices. Enemy-control spells do not make another human player obey you.

Restoration

Vanilla: healing, wards and undead defense. Mysticism broadens healing and support. Healing Hands, Heal Other and Grand Healing are our first player-healing options.

Find spell tomes through the usual vendors. Try direct spells first and read the tooltip. Staff builds are welcome, but extra staff skill experience from a separate mod is not part of this setup.

A healing effect is not a completed party test

Skyrim Together has support for ordinary health-restoring spells and the named buffs Courage, Rally and Call to Arms. Their exact behavior with this pack is not tested yet. Healing experience, perk scaling and custom auras need separate checks. An undead-healing spell is not automatically a player heal.

Magic we are holding

Skip Mark, Recall and all corpse-reanimation spells. A conjured skeleton is different from raising a body. Use only the main Mysticism package: the NPC spell-distribution addon and the optional Jump, Survival, Ordinator and Slower Unlocks files are outside this setup.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

If you cannot save, your latest progress may be lost. Tell the group when you can. You do not need to stay for a recap.

F2 is the F two key. This exit routine is not a proven crash fix. A full rehearsal with this party is not tested yet.
