Your character, our adventure

Gear, gold and a place to call home

Your character is saved on your own computer. Your pack and gold are personal. A synchronized chest does not mean everyone gets a separate copy, and a website ledger does not move an item.

Gear and loot

Take turns with shared loot. Offer a useful upgrade before selling it. Leave quest items to the leader. If an item disappears or duplicates, stop handling it and tell the host.

Gold

There is no automatic party purse. Shared combat experience is not shared money. The configured percentage death penalty is zero, but crime fines can still cost gold.

Check your home storage

Home synchronization is off. Recognized vanilla and DLC home containers are intended to stay local to each player, so a friend's chest is not a delivery box for yours. Test the exact chest with a cheap item, save, and check it after reconnecting before trusting it with valuables. This is not a blanket privacy or permanence guarantee.

A meeting place is not a bank

A home can be our social headquarters while each player uses tested personal storage. Shared containers are not a permanent server bank. Guild rooms, modded houses, outdoor barrels and floor piles need separate checks. Empty a room before renovations: furnishings can replace or remove containers.

Giving something to a friend: not tested yet

Try one cheap item first. Use an ordinary, unowned container outside a player home. Open its full inventory menu, put the agreed item in, then close it. Your friend opens it and takes that item. Both check their packs, save and later confirm the result after reconnecting. Do not trade by dropping items on the floor.

Use one person at a time and stop if counts disagree. Gold, custom potions, crafted gear and QuickLoot transfers are not tested yet on this setup. Never make a priceless item the first experiment or use a temporary trade container as long-term storage.

Getting a city home

Earn the city's permission through its quests, then buy an available home from the steward. Budget for furnishings as well as the house. The fixed leader handles prerequisite quest dialogue; do not split up to repeat the quest chain.

Building a Hearthfire home

After earning permission, a plot costs 5,000 gold before construction and furnishings. Choose a layout at the drafting table and build at the carpenter's bench with gathered or bought materials. Matching construction across the party is not tested yet.

Check the first purchase together

Record the buyer's payment, keys and access, then check what each friend sees. Four-player purchasing, furniture and ownership flags are not tested yet. Do not assume one purchase gives everybody a house, or that each person must repeat the payment.

Proposed ownership compact

Table etiquette for the group to agree on. If adopted, this is our agreement, not the game's rules. It is not recorded as accepted yet.

  • Your character, gear and gold are yours. No spawning, duplicating or reload-farming.
  • Offer real upgrades before selling. Prioritize someone who can use one now; alternate ties. The leader gets no bonus.
  • Only the fixed leader handles quest NPCs and quest items, following the play guide.
  • Shared costs are voluntary and have a named purpose. A pledge is not delivered gold.
  • A shared stash means named people holding earmarked supplies. Use separately tested personal home storage.
  • Earn homes normally. Name the buyer as the social owner and agree on guest use.
  • Protect an absent player's belongings and story opportunities.

Going deeper is optional: name the company, choose a home goal, divide crafting interests, or appoint a quartermaster. Record a supply's purpose, actual holder and whether it is pledged, held or spent. These are roleplay agreements, not systems the game enforces. Crafted-item transfers need testing before one person becomes everyone's supplier.

Marriage, adoption, shared building and horse ownership after reconnecting are not tested yet in this party. Earned outfits can become adventure goals; installing an extra costume mod still needs collection review.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

If you cannot save, your latest progress may be lost. Tell the group when you can. You do not need to stay for a recap.

F2 is the F two key. This exit routine is not a proven crash fix. A full rehearsal with this party is not tested yet.
