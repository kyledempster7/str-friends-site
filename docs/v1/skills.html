Skills and races: familiar foundations, new choices

There are still eighteen skills and no fixed classes. Better Vanilla Perks changes the existing trees. Imperious replaces racial abilities. Every race can learn every skill.

Follow the shared collection

These are the selected perk, race and spell mods for our campaign. Install the collection revision the group is using. A mod being mentioned here is not permission to add it yourself.

Read the current perk tooltip before spending a point. Familiar names can have changed effects. Pick a few skills that support your role; you do not need to invest in every skill you use.

In vanilla Skyrim, each race has a small set of advantages and powers. Imperious replaces that package with different traits, attributes and a power unlocked through a racial objective. Do not add the old and new bonuses together. Some changes also affect NPCs.

The perk and power shortlist to skip

Hold means do not use it in this campaign until the group changes the rule after testing.

  • Strong Reflexes rank 2 at Block 50. Rank 1 at Block 30 is a different, allowed effect.
  • Steady Hand rank 1 at Archery 40, and the rest of that branch.
  • Dark Souls 2 and its exploding reanimated corpses.
  • Dragonborn's spectral drum at Speech rank 4 and Dremora Merchant at rank 5.
  • Torch-assisted automatic unlocking and any use of extra summon capacity beyond one active summon per player.
  • Imperious powers: Red Sand Dance, Contingency, Beast Tongue and Spirit Walk.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

If you cannot save, your latest progress may be lost. Tell the group when you can. You do not need to stay for a recap.

F2 is the F two key. This exit routine is not a proven crash fix. A full rehearsal with this party is not tested yet.
