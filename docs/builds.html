Play someone you would not play alone

Four friends make room for specialists

Solo Skyrim asks you to solve nearly every problem yourself. With friends, you can spend more of your character on one thing you enjoy. These are flexible build ideas, not fixed classes or proven multiplayer combinations.

The field healer or paladin

Make bringing everyone home your best skill. Start with Restoration and a way to protect yourself. A paladin adds a weapon and shield, but switching between fighting and healing takes attention.

  • Give: a reliable place to retreat for healing.
  • Ask: interrupt enemies chasing me while I cast.
  • Start with ordinary robes or steel armor. A legendary sword is a future adventure, not a requirement.

The two-handed guardian

Use a greatsword, heavy armor and well-timed blocks or bashes to make space. Sometimes the best move is leaving your own duel to protect a friend.

  • Give: breathing room at the front of the fight.
  • Ask: cover enemy casters and keep a retreat lane clear.
  • There is no guaranteed taunt. Two-handed blocking does not grant shield-only defenses.

The stealth ranger

Scout one corner ahead, call the dangerous target, then take an agreed opening shot. Once discovered, keep moving and shooting; the party cannot promise every arrow a sneak bonus.

  • Give: a route, a warning and focused ranged damage.
  • Ask: a short quiet scouting window, then help if I am chased.
  • Leather or hide armor and an ordinary bow establish the character immediately.

The Gandalf-style wizard

A travelling mentor with light, fire, a staff when earned, and a little backup healing. Start with Destruction and Alteration; add support as resources allow.

  • Give: ranged magic and a calm second pair of eyes.
  • Ask: protect my casting space and tell me before the scout wants darkness.
  • Robes, a hood and earned equipment are enough. Movie costumes require a separately approved collection change.

The gravewarden

Heal the living while one conjured helper buys time. This is Restoration first, Conjuration second. Summoning a creature and raising an existing corpse are different mechanics.

  • Give: recovery plus a distraction on a spare flank.
  • Ask: leave room for the helper and do not expect an army.
  • Corpse raising stays on hold. A darker character story does not lift a gameplay restriction. Check the rules before choosing a summon or perk.

The storyteller or controller

A skald can use Speech, Restoration and a weapon. A controller can use enemy control and a dependable damage option. Friends make room for support that would feel slow alone.

  • Give: a shared plan, useful support, or time to recover.
  • Ask: respect a control call and give me a turn in decisions.
  • Instrument performances and party buff songs are not promised features of this setup. The quest face must still be the fixed leader.

Leave room to enjoy the role

Keep one useful fallback for quiet moments or resistant enemies. After two sessions, check whether your specialty is fun. A healer can add damage; a guardian can change tactics. Discuss a major role change so the party still has recovery covered.

Healing experience, perk bonuses on human allies, added summons and these full teamwork sequences are not tested yet in this party. Read the installed spell and perk descriptions, then check Can I use this? before spending points.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

If you cannot save, your latest progress may be lost. Tell the group when you can. You do not need to stay for a recap.

F2 is the F two key. This exit routine is not a proven crash fix. A full rehearsal with this party is not tested yet.
