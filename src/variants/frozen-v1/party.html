Make each other's jobs easier

A party, not four solo runs

Tank means making space. Healer means keeping people effective. Damage dealer means finishing the threat. Anyone can notice trouble, help a friend or suggest the next adventure.

One party leader per playthrough

Keep the same leader for this entire campaign. The leader handles quest NPCs, quest items and quest scenes, and enters locations first. Everyone can fight bosses, solve puzzles and help choose the route. Do not rotate the mechanical leader for someone's story turn.

Pass the story spotlight instead: one friend proposes two destinations, everyone gets a say, and the same leader handles the quest interactions. Use a separate campaign with separate characters if you want a different leader.

On me

An enemy is chasing the speaker. They move toward the group; the frontliner turns to intercept. Nobody assumes enemies stay on the tank forever.

Healing here

The healer names a safe position. Hurt friends come within reach. Healing is not a promised resurrection button, and a wizard's ward is not a guaranteed shield for everyone.

Caster first

The ranger and wizard focus the named threat while the frontline makes room. A clear target is usually more useful than four people chasing four enemies.

Typing counts. We wait before decisions.

Open Skyrim Together with F2 or Right Ctrl, click its message field, type and send with Enter. Ordinary messages use global chat. Use /party READY for party chat or /local Hello for local chat. These commands exist in version 1.8.2; delivery and readability on all four players' screens are not tested yet.

  • READY: I am ready for the next step.
  • HOLD: find safety and wait before starting anything.
  • HELP: I need help now; add a location if possible.
  • BRB: I need a short break. This does not pause the game.
  • Let the person typing finish before a vote or a pull. Someone should watch chat and acknowledge messages. Automatic text-to-speech is not a promised feature.

Use the same agreed mod collection. True Directional Movement locks onto hostiles, so aim ally heals yourself. Skyrim Souls RE keeps menus live, so prepare favourites before combat. Player friendly fire is off in this setup; neutral characters still need care around wide swings and spells. New mods belong in group review before installation.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

If you cannot save, your latest progress may be lost. Tell the group when you can. You do not need to stay for a recap.

F2 is the F two key. This exit routine is not a proven crash fix. A full rehearsal with this party is not tested yet.
