A day in the life

From joining to calling it a night

One shared adventure, with room for a late arrival, a typed message, or an early goodbye.

Before your first join

Use the collection revision the group has chosen. Create your campaign character offline and finish Helgen, or the Helgen Express start, before connecting. Save outside the intro. Keep separate characters for separate campaigns.

  1. Open the Skyrim Together launch shortcut and load your latest campaign save.
  2. Press F2, or Right Ctrl, to open Skyrim Together. Choose Connect and use the connection details sent privately by the host.
  3. Wait for the connection confirmation. Check Player Manager: you need to be in the campaign's party, with its usual leader.
  4. If no party exists, let the fixed leader create it. If you are outside the party, ask for help joining it before advancing any quest.
  5. Meet at the agreed location. Prepare a healing option and your favourite tools, then type or say READY.

Example: arriving ten minutes late

Join, check the party, and ask where to meet. Let everyone regroup before the next quest conversation. Do not catch up by completing objectives alone.

Example: the first dungeon room

The leader enters first. The ranger checks the next corner and calls a target. Everyone answers READY. The frontliner intercepts, the wizard adds damage, and the healer keeps a retreat route open.

Example: someone needs a minute

Type HOLD or BRB. The group moves to safety and waits. Menus and a chat message do not pause the danger.

A normal goodbye

Find safety and tell the party you are leaving. Save your own character and let the save finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Everyone saves their own progress; the server is not a backup of your character.

Save during the adventure too, especially before major scenes. If progress disagrees or something breaks, stop and tell the host what happened. Do not reload an older save while connected or try to repair the quest by yourself.

Real life comes first

I have to go NOW

Save. Disconnect. Go.

Save if possible. Let it finish. Press F2, choose Disconnect, then Proceed. Quit the game. Quitting may still crash; no fix is proven.

If you cannot save, your latest progress may be lost. Tell the group when you can. You do not need to stay for a recap.

F2 is the F two key. This exit routine is not a proven crash fix. A full rehearsal with this party is not tested yet.
